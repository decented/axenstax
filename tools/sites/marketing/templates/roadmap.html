A lot of this is already in the game. Here's what you can do right now, and what we're only dreaming about for later.

Ready today

You can do this now

The browser game is a quick, private taster: no account, no sign-in, and your worlds stay on your device. The desktop app is the whole game.

🤝 Play with friends (desktop app)

Build together on a LAN, set up your own dedicated server, or invite a friend from another house straight into a world you host at home. It runs directly between your computers, and strangers can't just wander in.

🌾 Farm, fish and cook

Till fields, grow crops, go fishing, compost, and keep animals. Breed them and see how their traits pass on.

🐺 Raise a crew of companions

Tame wolves and other pets, ride steeds, keep bees, and breed your own animals. They follow, sit and fight at your side.

🏗️ Build it your way

Capture a build and drop it anywhere, follow a step-by-step build-along or hire a Builder villager to put it up for you, and use stairs, slabs, doors and panes. Reskin and reshape blocks and creatures in the Workshop, and paint your own skin or bring one over from Minecraft.

⚡ Power things up

Wire up switches, batteries and lamps, turn a stream into power with a water wheel, or catch the wind with a windmill.

🏁 Take on Trials

Timed races and explorer challenges, plus Experiences like Hash Dash and Satori Rush.

💻 Get the desktop app

The Linux app is ready now and updates itself in place. Take your browser worlds with you in a single file. Windows and Mac can be built from source for now.

Not built yet

Ideas for later

These are things we'd love to get to. None is promised and none has a date.

🌌 Linked worlds

One home with several worlds you can step between through portals.

🚜 A deeper farm

Draft animals, livestock and a food economy that goes well past the first few tiers.

🏪 Richer player-run shops

Markets, auctions and bounties that you and your friends run on your own terms.

🚂 Rail and power, further

Climbing rails, freight and cart tiers, and a bigger electrical world.

🎙️ Voice and a second screen

Talking to the people you're building with, and a handheld as a controller or map.

🎬 Replays and your own Experiences

Camera paths and replays of your best builds, and a way to design challenges of your own.

📱 More places to play

Windows and Mac installers, and an Android version.

It's playable right now

Open it in your browser and start mining, no account needed. When you want the whole game, grab the desktop app.
