# Test sheet — Feel wave: controls card, sound & survival (2026-10-05)

**Build:** the next build after v0.2.27. This sheet was written while the code
was still uncompiled (audit wave W2) — check the version line in Settings
before you start, and tell us which build you tested.
**Needs:** one machine for everything. Test the **web build** (play.axenstax.com
in Chrome) AND the **native app** where a step says so. A phone or tablet is
needed for the touch steps in §1 (optional but useful). Survival worlds unless
a step says Creative. Use `/give` and `/tp` freely to set things up.

Design / spec: **Spec 05** — §1.5 Fall damage, §1.5.1 Drowning, §6.7 death
screen, §8.5 difficulty table, §11 audio, §11.10 controls card.

**How to report:** play, then jot a note per line — ✓ works / ✗ doesn't /
🤔 felt off.

## 1. Controls card

A one-time "Controls" card for brand-new players, plus a button to bring it
back. Wipe the site data (or use a private window) so the web game thinks it is
a new device. On native, delete `settings.json` next to `worlds/` — or just
note that it will not show if you have played before.

- [ ] **First spawn:** make a new world. When the loading screen ends, a
      **Controls** card appears in the middle of the screen. The mouse pointer
      is free and you **cannot** walk or look until you dismiss it.
- [ ] The card lists keys in groups (Moving / Building and items / Menus and
      maps / Extras). Read every line against the real game — press each key.
      **Does every row do what the card says?** Flag any row that is wrong.
- [ ] Dismiss it with **Got it**. You can move and look straight away and the
      pointer is captured again.
- [ ] Make another new world (or reload the page / restart the app). The card
      does **not** appear again.
- [ ] Do it again, but dismiss the card with **Esc** (and a third time with
      **Enter**). Esc must close the card, **not** open the pause menu behind
      it.
- [ ] **Pause menu → Controls** (Esc, then the Controls button). The same card
      comes back over the world. Close it with **Got it** — you are back in the
      game, not still paused.
- [ ] Do the same from a **Trial's** pause menu (J, start any Trial, Esc) —
      there is a Controls button there too.
- [ ] Press **H** in a Creative or Survival free-play world: the help sheet now
      starts with the movement basics (W A S D, Space, Shift, sprint, 1-9, E, Q
      and so on), then the T / M / J / N lines. Does it read clearly and fit on
      the screen?
- [ ] **Touch (phone / tablet / touchscreen Chromebook):** the card shows the
      **touch** layout (joystick, Jump / Sneak / Break / Place buttons, number
      slots, Inventory, Pause) and **no** keyboard keys. Tap **Got it**. The
      Pause button, then Controls, reopens it. On a small phone screen, can you
      scroll the list and still reach **Got it**?
- [ ] A **desktop with a mouse** that also has a touchscreen: which layout did
      it show? (Either is acceptable — just tell us which, and whether it was
      the one you wanted.)
- [ ] 🤔 Is there a key you use that is **missing** from the card? Is there
      anything on it you would never need?

### Loading tips

- [ ] Watch the tips on two or three loading screens. **None** should ask you
      to send feedback or say "tell us" / "report" / "needs testing". None
      should mention a gamepad. (New tips: flying, sprinting, finding items,

## 2. Sound (web first, then native)

Seven sounds exist: **break, place, footstep, explosion, thunder, gem pickup,
Genesis fanfare** (the first Satori in a world). They are synthesised — beeps
and noise, not recordings — so judge them as "does it help?", not "is it
pretty?".

- [ ] **Web, Chrome:** load a world, then **click or press a key** (the card's
      Got it counts). Break and place a block. You should hear a short
      noise-pop on break and a short tone on place. *The very first click can be
      silent — that is the browser's autoplay rule. Anything after it should
      make noise.* Was the first click silent, and was every later one audible?
- [ ] Walk, then sprint: footsteps tick along (slower walking, quicker
      sprinting) and are not annoying.
- [ ] `/give` yourself a **Blasting Keg** and set it off: boom. Wait for a
      thunderstorm (or run the `catch-the-wind` Trial, which locks one): thunder
      rumble.
- [ ] Get a **Satori** (mine one deep down, or `/give` it): a bright chime on a normal pickup; the
      first one in a world plays the four-note rising fanfare.
- [ ] **Safari / Firefox** if you have them: does sound start after your first
      click? (Safari is the strictest browser about this.)
- [ ] **Settings** (lobby *or* pause menu → Graphics): there is a **Sound**
      section with a **Volume** slider (0-100%) and **Mute all sound**.
- [ ] Drag Volume to about 30%: sounds get clearly quieter, live, without
      leaving the panel. At 0% they are silent. At 100% they are as loud as
      before this build.
- [ ] Tick **Mute all sound**: silence. Untick: sound returns at the volume you
      set.
- [ ] **Persistence:** set 30% and mute on, reload the page (web) or restart
      the app (native). The slider and the checkbox are still where you left
      them, and the game really is muted / quiet **before** you open Settings.
- [ ] Change the volume from the **lobby** Settings (before entering a world),
      then enter a world: still applied?
- [ ] Pick a **graphics preset** (Potato / High …): your volume and mute are
      **not** reset.
- [ ] **Native:** repeat the break / place / footstep / explosion check and the
      volume + mute steps. Did anything sound different from the last build?
- [ ] 🤔 **Feel question:** is 100% too loud for a classroom? Should the
      default be lower (say 70%)? Are the sounds too harsh, too long, or too
      repetitive when mining a big wall?

## 3. Fall damage

Survival world, normal difficulty unless stated. `/tp` up and `/give` blocks to
build drops.

- [ ] **Safe height:** drop **3 blocks** onto stone — **no damage**.
- [ ] Drop **4 blocks** — lose **1 HP** (half a heart). 5 blocks — **2 HP**,
      and so on, **1 HP per block beyond 3**. (Count the hearts and tell us if
      any fall is off by one.)
- [ ] A tall drop of about **10 blocks** — roughly 7 HP.
- [ ] Land in **water** from a great height (20+ blocks): **no damage**, any
      depth. Also try a shallow 1-block puddle.
- [ ] Land on a **hay bale** from about 13 blocks: you take about **80% less**
      than the same fall onto stone (13 blocks onto stone is 10 HP; on hay it
      should be 2 HP).
- [ ] **Creative mode:** fall from any height — **immune**. (And flying then
      switching it off mid-air does not hurt.)
- [ ] **Armour:** wear armour and take a fall — fall damage is **not** reduced
      by armour (that is deliberate).
- [ ] Fall damage happens on **Peaceful** too (deliberate, like Minecraft).
- [ ] Jump up and down on the spot, walk off a 1-block step, climb and fall off
      a **ladder**: no stray damage. `/tp` to somewhere high: no fall damage
      from the teleport itself.
- [ ] 🤔 Does the "3 blocks is free" rule feel fair? Does a fall feel like it
      **needs a sound or a thud** (it has none right now)?

## 4. Drowning

- [ ] Dive underwater in Survival. A row of **10 bubbles** appears above the
      hunger bar. It does **not** show when you are on land or only wading.
- [ ] Stay down: the bubbles pop one by one over about **15 seconds**.
- [ ] At zero air you take **2 HP (one heart) every second**. Time it — is it
      about 1 hit per second?
- [ ] Swim up before you die: you breathe again in about **2 seconds**, the
      bubbles refill and the damage stops.
- [ ] Only your **head** matters: stand in water up to your chest — no bubbles
      drain.
- [ ] **Armour** does not stop drowning damage.
- [ ] **Creative mode:** the bubble bar never drains and you never drown.
- [ ] Drown on purpose: the **death screen says "You drowned"**.
- [ ] After you respawn, you have full lungs.
- [ ] 🤔 Is 15 seconds the right amount of air for kids? Did you notice the
      bubbles in time, or only the damage?

## 5. Difficulty levels

Pause menu → difficulty buttons. The same fight on each level, using a
Brigand or any other hostile mob (wait for night, or find one). Note the damage
per hit.

- [ ] **Peaceful:** hostile mobs do **not** attack you at all. (Bees, goats
      and sharks can still hurt you.)
- [ ] **Easy:** hostile hits do about **half** the damage of Normal (a bit
      more than half on small hits).
- [ ] **Normal:** the baseline.
- [ ] **Hard:** hostile hits do about **1.5×** Normal.
- [ ] **Starving** (empty hunger bar, wait): on **Easy** you stop losing health
      at 5 hearts; on **Normal** at half a heart (1 HP); on **Hard** starving
      can **kill** you; **Peaceful** stops at the old half-heart floor.
- [ ] Switch difficulty mid-world from the pause menu: it takes effect
      straight away.
- [ ] **Falls and drowning** hurt the same on every level (including Hard) —
      they are not scaled.
- [ ] 🤔 Do Easy / Normal / Hard feel **different enough**? Does Hard feel
      fair, or just annoying?

## 5a. Hurt feedback and the death screen

- [ ] Every hit that lands (fall, drowning, a mob) flashes a **red glow around
      the edges** of the screen for under half a second. It must **never**
      cover the whole screen in red, and it should not hide what you are
      doing.
- [ ] Die from a **fall**: the death screen says **"You fell from a high
      place"**.
- [ ] Die to a mob: **"Killed by a …"** with the right mob name (try an
      Ocelot — it should say "an Ocelot").
- [ ] Also try dying to **starvation**, **lava**, **fire** and an
      **explosion**: each gets its own line ("You starved", "You tried to swim
      in lava", "You burned to death", "You were blown up").
- [ ] Below the cause there is **"Your items are in a grave at x, y, z"**.
      Walk to those coordinates: the grave is there and has your things.
- [ ] Die in **Creative** or with **keep-inventory** on: no grave line (nothing
      was dropped).
- [ ] Die from a fall into a hole, respawn: you start at your spawn point and
      take **no** fall damage from the respawn.
- [ ] **Joined / multiplayer world:** die in someone else's world. Does your own
      screen show the right cause line?
- [ ] The death screen **no longer auto-respawns**: it stays up until you
      choose. Wait 10 seconds and confirm it is still there with the cause
      line and grave coordinates readable.
- [ ] Click the **Respawn button**: you respawn at your spawn point.
- [ ] Press **Enter** (keyboard seat): you respawn.
- [ ] Press **A on that seat's gamepad** (split-screen: each seat only
      responds to its own pad): that seat respawns.
- [ ] 🤔 Was there any moment you were stuck on the death screen with no
      obvious way out?
