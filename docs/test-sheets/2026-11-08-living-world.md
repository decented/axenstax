# Living world v0.2.30: things to try

For Axolittle's play of build 0.2.30 (main 46b5717f). The known limits behind the rough bits are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-living-world-tier1.md` §6.3.

```
Things to try: the living world (v0.2.30)

The idea: places come alive or go grey depending on how you treat them. There are no numbers for you, just colour, sound and life.

1. Watch a place wake up
   Walk from a meadow or forest into somewhere you've dug out or built over. The colour should drain where it's bare and get richer where it's green and full of life. The grass sways, birds sing by day, crickets chirp at night, and there are pollen bits in the air and fireflies after dark. Plant things, mix lots of different plants, and see if the place perks up. It changes slowly, over a few minutes, not straight away.

2. Hold a Feast
   Make a Feast Table: three planks on top, then stick, bread, stick underneath. Place it, hold some food and right-click the table. It shares the food out to everyone nearby, and villagers count too, so you need company. The table turns into a Feast marker that remembers how many came. Places near a feast liven up.

3. Make a Commons
   A Commons Stone is eight cobblestone round a sapling. Place it to mark open, shared ground. It can't overlap someone's Plot.

4. Play together
   Two players in the same area also liven it up a bit. Try a Feast with a friend on a LAN game.

5. The Grey Quarry (new Trial, flower icon)
   A stripped, grey quarry you have to bring back to life. It unlocks once a place in your own world goes properly grey (dig out a big patch and stay there), or after you've done the Power Plant trial. Plant lots of different things and bring animals in (you get saplings, seeds, a hoe and Leads). It ends when the place has stayed alive for a while.

6. For grown-ups
   Settings has a "Grown-up panel (F3)" switch, off by default. Turn it on, then press F3. In the top right it shows the Tally (what's been mined, placed, crafted, smelted and traded this session) and how alive the world is overall.

Known rough bits (tell us, but don't worry about them):
- Play in Survival to see places go grey. In Creative nothing ever goes properly grey, so there the Grey Quarry only unlocks after Power Plant.
- Worlds saved in this build won't open in the older 0.2.29.
- Two players may see slightly different colour in the same spot.
- A place's liveliness from a feast or a visit fades if you quit and reload.
- A Commons Stone doesn't stop anyone breaking blocks yet.
- A Feast on your own does nothing. It needs other players or villagers nearby.
- The Grey Quarry kit has a Feast Table, but a Feast needs guests and the quarry has none, so it does nothing there on your own.
- Trying the Grey Quarry again carries on from how you left it, not from grey.
- How long the Grey Quarry takes depends on how fast the trees happen to grow.
- The "Somewhere grey is waiting" hint doesn't show up.
- No before/after pictures yet.
- If you join someone else's game, your own world can't unlock the Grey Quarry from there.
- On a dedicated server the grown-up panel shows 0 for crafted, smelted and traded, and auctions aren't counted anywhere.
- The colours, sounds and timings are first guesses. Say what feels off and we'll tune it.
```
