# 27 October 2026 — mob simulation and dedicated animal life

Use disposable survival worlds. Record the tested commit, native platform,
host mode and tester below. Manual checks remain pending until ticked.
Standalone is still the default; use `--solo-hosted` for the local authority
comparison. This slice changes no wire or save version.

Commit / platform / mode / tester:

## Local comparison

- [ ] In standalone and `--solo-hosted`, ride a horse forwards, turn, jump
      and sneak to dismount. The horse and rider move together in both modes.
- [ ] In both modes, take a nearby hostile mob's hit while standing and
      while mounted. Check health, knockback and armour wear; one hit must
      not land twice or disappear while riding.
- [ ] Feed a breeding pair. One baby and one local breeding notification
      appear. Watch a chicken's eggs and a Nostrich's products for duplicate
      drops, and use a Lead to pull an animal along.

## Two native machines on a lending host

- [ ] Join a signed host, then feed a pair together. The host and joiner
      see one baby, and credit belongs to the players who fed its parents.
- [ ] Tame a pet as the signed joiner, attach a Lead to an animal, move
      away and issue a pet command. Both screens show the same animals.
- [ ] Ride past the other player, steer, then dismount. Both screens agree
      about the rider and mount; repeat while a hostile mob can reach them.
- [ ] Join or respawn near hostile mobs. During the first 15 seconds,
      without attacking, they do not target or hurt the joiner. Include a
      shark in water, a hyena at night, a provoked bear and a nearby blasting keg. After the
      protection window, ordinary targeting and damage resume.

## Operator-run dedicated server

- [ ] As a signed joiner, feed, tame, use a Lead and issue a pet command.
      None is refused because the server cannot simulate animal life.
- [ ] Save and restart the server, then reconnect with the same identity.
      The tamed pet keeps its owner and follows that player again.
- [ ] Open a disposable older world containing a local-seat pet. A joiner
      occupying the same numbered slot does not acquire that pet, its
      follow behaviour or a perched parrot.
