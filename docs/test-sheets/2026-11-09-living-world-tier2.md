# Living world v0.2.31: things to try

For Axolittle's play of build 0.2.31. It adds to the v0.2.30 sheet (`2026-11-08-living-world.md`); everything there still applies. The known limits behind the rough bits are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-living-world-tier2.md` §13.

```
Things to try: the living world, part 2 (v0.2.31)

Same idea as last time: places come alive or go grey depending on how you treat them. This build is about people: building together, using a place, sharing, and leaving things for others.

1. A Commons now protects what people build
   Put down a Commons Stone (eight cobblestone round a sapling) and build inside it. Then get a friend to try breaking your blocks. They should be told "Someone else built this." Anyone can still break the Commons Stone itself.

2. Wear a path
   In Survival, walk the same bit of grass back and forth lots of times. It turns into a worn dirt path. Paths only come from feet, so the places you really use get them. Satoshi says something the first time you stand on one.

3. Build together
   A place gets livelier when more than one person has built in it. Build a bit each in the same spot with a friend and see if the colour comes up.

4. Live somewhere
   A place you keep coming back to, and sleep in, slowly gets livelier over the days. It needs a proper build there too (a small house is plenty).

5. Plan plaques count
   Build something from a Plan, then build it again. The plaque now says how many times it has been built in this world, and how many more were built from it.

6. Blight
   Plant a big field of just one crop: 64 or more, with almost nothing else mixed in. After a while some of it goes grey and the grey spreads. Pull the grey ones up and mix other plants in. Satoshi has a line about it.

7. Plots for friends who join
   If you join someone else's game, you can now put down your own Plot Marker and own that Plot. If the owner doesn't come back for 60 real days of the world being hosted, the Plot passes to the village and turns into a Commons.

8. The Commons Shelf
   Recipe: three planks on top, then stick, chest, stick underneath. It's a shared chest anyone can use. A full shelf makes a place livelier. Empty a full one in a day and the place dims a little until someone fills it up again.

9. The Site Board
   Recipe: stick, plank, stick in each of the three rows. Right-click it to pick jobs from a list (plant trees, dig a well, build a bridge and so on) and tick them off when they're done. Ticking a job gives the place a little boost, once a day per board.

10. Villager moods
   Villagers in a lively place sometimes give off a happy puff. In a grey, quiet place they give off a grey one.

11. For grown-ups
   The F3 grown-up panel now has four more lines: Life, Ideas, People together and Time. They're for the whole world, never per person.

12. Playing together
   If you join someone else's game you now see the built-together and lived-in colour too, not just the plants.

Fixed: crops and saplings sometimes stopped growing after you saved and reloaded. They should grow normally now.

Known rough bits (tell us, but don't worry about them):
- Worlds saved in this build won't open in 0.2.30.
- A block pushed by a piston, or one that falls, can be broken by anyone.
- If a crop inside a Commons goes grey, anyone can pull it up.
- Nothing cures a grey crop. Pull it up.
- A field of one crop that crosses a chunk edge counts as two smaller fields, so it may not go grey.
- Paths can wear across someone's Plot or a Commons.
- On a LAN game, typing /gamemode creative still counts as Survival for paths and blight.
- If you join a game, your grown-up panel shows a dash for Ideas and Time.
- When you join a game and your Plot Marker isn't allowed, it just disappears without a message.
- Playing on your own doesn't count toward a Plot passing on. Only time the world is hosted for others counts.
- Satoshi's path and grey-crop lines come back if you quit and rejoin.
- A shelf stocked and emptied in the same day it started nearly empty doesn't count as looted.
- The Site Board needs a mouse. It doesn't work with only a gamepad.
- Only the nearest 24 villagers show moods.
- A Site Board can give its boost again the same day after a reload.
```
