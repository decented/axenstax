# Commons

A **Commons** is common ground: a patch of land that belongs to everyone who plays on it. Anyone can build there, and what they build is looked after.

## Make a Commons Stone

Craft it at a **Crafting Table**. Put **eight Cobblestone** in a ring and **any sapling** in the middle:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Cobblestone** | **Cobblestone** | **Cobblestone** |
| **Cobblestone** | **Sapling** | **Cobblestone** |
| **Cobblestone** | **Cobblestone** | **Cobblestone** |

Any kind of sapling works. In Creative mode the Commons Stone is in the creative inventory.

Place the stone where you want the Commons. A line appears: **"Commons declared. Anyone may build here."**

## How big it is

A Commons covers **33 by 33 blocks**, with the stone in the middle. That is the same size as a Plot.

- A Commons and a Plot cannot overlap. If you try, you see **"This land is claimed."** and the block does not go down. That works both ways round: a Plot Marker will not go down on ground a Commons already covers.
- You can have as many Commons as you like, as long as they stay off other people's Plots.

## What it does

- **Anyone may build there.** There is no owner to ask.
- **It gives that place a small lift in [bloom](bloom.md)**, and players together there lift it a little faster. Common ground says "this place is shared."
- **Your build is looked after.** In Survival, inside a Commons, nobody can break, build over, or use a tool or bucket on a block that **another player** built. If someone tries they see **"Someone else built this."** The message never says who.

## What is still free to break

- **Natural blocks**, like the original stone, trees and dirt.
- **Blocks nobody is recorded as having placed.** That means blocks from before this feature, blocks placed by guests who are not signed in, and blocks that have been moved by a piston or have fallen. See **[Who Built It](who-built-it.md)**.
- **The Commons Stone itself.** Anyone can take a Commons down. A Commons nobody could remove would be a claim, not a shared place.
- Everything in **Creative** mode. Creative ignores this protection, as it does for Plots.

Fire, blasts, pistons and falling blocks are not stopped by a Commons. It protects against a player's hand, not against the world.

## When a friend moves on

A signed-in friend can have their own Plot on a world you host. If they have not visited for **60 real days**, their Plot **passes to the village as a Commons**. The marker becomes a Commons Stone. The blocks they built stay, but they become nobody's, so anyone can tend them.

- The days only count while the world is being hosted for friends. Days when the world was not running do not count.
- Your own household's Plot, the ones for the players sitting at your computer, never passes on.
- The first time you stand in such a place, Satoshi says: "Someone built this and moved on. It's the village's now."

## Limits

- Commons are found from the parts of the world that are loaded, so a far-off Commons shows up when you get near.
- If a friend's placement is refused as claimed on a world you host, the block just does not stand. There is no message on their side.
- Commons protection works from a record kept by the computer that holds the world. See **[Who Built It](who-built-it.md)** for what that record is and is not.
