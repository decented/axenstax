# Tools Reference

This is the lookup page for **how good each tool is** — how long it lasts, how hard it hits, how fast it mines, and which pickaxe tier can harvest which blocks.

For the **crafting shapes**, see [Crafting](crafting.md). For the **block tier gate** in more detail, see [Blocks & Mining](blocks-and-mining.md).

## The tier ladder

Most tools come in five tiers. Higher tier = more durability, faster mining, and (for weapons) more damage. You swap the **head material** to change tier — same shape every time.

| Tier | Head material |
|---|---|
| **Wood** | Planks (any wood) |
| **Stone** | Cobblestone |
| **Iron** | Iron Ingot |
| **Diamond** | Diamond |
| **Satori** | Satori — end-game; the Bitcoin gem |

Five tools have **no ladder** — there's just one version: Flint & Steel, Shears, Fishing Rod, Slingshot, Eraser.

## Attack damage

How much each weapon takes off in a single hit, by tier (Wood / Stone / Iron / Diamond / Satori).

| Tool | Wood | Stone | Iron | Diamond | Satori |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Sword** | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| **Axe** | 7 | 7 | 7 | 9 | 10 |
| **Bow** (melee) | 1 | 1 | 1.5 | 2 | 2.5 |
| **Hoe** | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Everything else | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

The Bow does its real damage through **arrows**, not melee: every fired arrow does a flat **4 damage**, no matter which tier of Bow fired it. A higher-tier Bow lasts longer and hits harder in melee (see the table above), but doesn't make its arrows hit harder.

The **Sword** is your best per-swing weapon; the **Axe** hits harder but swings count more against its durability. The **Hoe** is a farming tool, not a weapon — it always does 1.

## Mining speed

A tool only mines fast when it's the **best tool for that block**. Used on anything else, it mines at the slowest rate (×1).

| Tier | Speed (best-tool only) |
|---|---:|
| Wood | ×2 |
| Stone | ×4 |
| Iron | ×6 |
| Diamond | ×8 |
| Satori | ×9 |

**Break time = block hardness ÷ speed × 20 ticks** (20 ticks = 1 second). So a harder block, or a slower tool, takes longer.

Which tool is "best" for each job:

| Tool | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Pickaxe** | Stone and ore |
| **Shovel** | Dirt, sand, gravel, snow |
| **Axe** | Logs, planks, Crafting Table |

## The mining-tier gate

A block only **drops** when you break it with a pickaxe of the **required tier or higher**. Break it with a weaker pickaxe and the block still goes — but you get **nothing**.

| Pickaxe tier | Unlocks drops from |
|---|---|
| **Wood** | Stone, Cobblestone, Sandstone, Coal Ore, Coal Block |
| **Stone** | Iron Ore, Iron Block, the pure-deepslate family, Deepslate Coal Ore, Deepslate Iron Ore, Magnesium Ore, Brimstone, Nitre Ore |
| **Iron** | Diamond Ore, Diamond Block, Satori Block, Deepslate Diamond Ore |
| **(none)** | Dirt, sand, wood, glass — no tier needed |

⚠️ **No block currently needs a Diamond or Satori pickaxe to mine.** But digging up the raw **Satori gem** itself, deep underground, needs a **Diamond-or-better** pickaxe — see [Blocks & Mining](blocks-and-mining.md).

## A note on two recipes

Two single-tier recipes are easy to get wrong, so to be clear:

- ✅ **Shears** = two Iron Ingots **stacked in one column** (a vertical 1×2). *Not* a diagonal.
- ✅ **Flint & Steel** = **Flint on top, Iron Ingot below** (a vertical 1×2). *Not* "any arrangement".

The grids for these (and every other tool) are on the [Crafting](crafting.md) page.
