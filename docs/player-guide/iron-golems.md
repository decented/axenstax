# Knights

The village's defender. A Knight is on your side — it fights raiders so you and the villagers don't have to. Friend if you're a villager. Friend if you're a player. A problem only if you're a bandit, a Bear, or a Hyena that wandered too close.

> This page used to cover the Iron Golem. The Iron Golem was removed in the 2026-05-24 roster cleanup; the **Knight** is now the sole village defender and replaces it everywhere.

## What they do

The Knight patrols the village, staying near the village's anchor (the campfire hearth). When a **hostile mob** (a Brigand, Marauder, Berserker, Bear, or Hyena) gets close, the Knight moves to intercept and fights it off.

Knights are **not hostile toward players**. They won't attack you — they're there to defend the village against raiders, and during a raid they fight on your side.

## How they spawn

The village automatically gets a Knight when **both** conditions are met:

1. **≥ 3 employed villagers** within 24 blocks of the village hearth. Unemployed villagers don't count toward this — a village of 6 idle villagers gets no Knight until at least 3 of them claim a workstation.
2. **≥ 5 houses** built (the procgen layout has 3–6 per village, so most villages qualify once 3 villagers are employed).

The spawn check runs once per second. As soon as the village qualifies, a Knight appears. Most alpha villages have one.

A village isn't capped at one Knight forever — it can get another roughly every **10 employed villagers** (so a thriving village of 20+ employed villagers can field two or more).

## Stats

A Knight has **60 HP** — tanky, built to outlast a raid — and hits for **8 damage** per landed blow. Don't pick a fight with one: it's not a loot piñata, and attacking the village's defender works against you.

## Tips

- **A village with a Knight is the safest place to sleep through the first night.** It deals with mobs that wander in.
- **Smoke pillars draw hostile mobs toward the village.** Near a defended village, that's free pest control — the Knight handles the wave.
- **If you're a streamer or a kid showing off:** stand inside a defended village at night and watch the Knight clear the bandits a lit campfire draws in. Good gameplay loop.
