# The Commons Shelf

A Commons Shelf is a shared chest for a whole place. Anyone can put things in and anyone can take things out. It has no owner and no lock. A shelf that is kept stocked is a sign that people look after the place, so **[bloom](bloom.md)** notices it.

## Make a Commons Shelf

Craft it at a **Crafting Table**. Three planks across the top, with a **wooden Chest** between two sticks underneath:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Plank** | **Plank** | **Plank** |
| **Stick** | **Chest** | **Stick** |

- Any three planks work, from any wood.
- It must be a plain **wooden Chest**. A copper chest does not work.
- In Creative mode the shelf is in the creative inventory.

You can place a shelf anywhere. It does not need a Commons Stone. Mine it with an axe and it drops itself, with its contents spilling out.

## What it does

It opens like a wooden chest, with the same 27 slots. Hoppers can fill it and empty it.

Bloom looks at **how many of its slots hold something**, not at how much is in each. A shelf with most slots in use lifts its place the most. One empty shelf adds nothing. If there are several shelves in one place, the fullest one counts.

## Looted

If somebody takes almost everything out in a single game day, the shelf is marked **looted**, and its place dims a little until it is restocked.

- A shelf can only be looted if it started the day at least a quarter full.
- It is looted when it has dropped to **half of what it started the day with**, or lower.
- It stops being looted once about **half its slots** are in use again.

A looted shelf does not name anyone, and it never blocks anyone. It is just a sign that the place has run down. Put things back and it recovers.

## Limits

- The shelf only counts **how full it is**. It never records who put things in or who took them out.
- **Creative** mode places stay vivid even with a looted shelf.
- The fullness shown follows the contents within a moment. A shelf nobody has opened yet counts as empty.
