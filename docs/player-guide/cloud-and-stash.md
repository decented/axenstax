# Moving Your Worlds

Your worlds live on the computer you made them on. In the desktop app they are saved in the app's own folder. In the browser they are saved inside that browser. To move a world to another computer, hand it to a friend, or keep a spare copy, you save it to a **file** and open the file somewhere else.

> **There is no cloud save.** Nothing in the game uploads your worlds anywhere. On the desktop app you may see a **"Keep in your Stash"** choice in a world's **More** menu, and a **"Save to your Stash"** choice when you create a world. Today those only remember your choice on that computer. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored anywhere else.

## The two kinds of file

| File | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `<name>.axeworld` | One world |
| `<date>-axenstax-profile.axeprofile` | **Every** world you saved in a browser, plus your Trials records |

## The buttons

Open **Your worlds** from the lobby.

| Where | Button | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Web** (browser) | **More** on a world, then **Download a backup** | That one world as `<name>.axeworld` |
| **Web** (browser) | **Save everything to a file** | One `.axeprofile` with all your browser worlds and your Trials records |
| **Web** (browser) | **Restore from a file** | Opens a world file you saved earlier |
| **Desktop app** | **More** on a world, then **Save a copy as a file…** | A Save dialog, so you pick where `<name>.axeworld` goes |
| **Desktop app** | **More** on a world, then **Save a copy to the transfer folder** | `<name>.axeworld` in the `world-transfer` folder |
| **Desktop app** | **Import**, then **Open a world file…** | Opens one `.axeworld` file |
| **Desktop app** | **Import**, then **Bring in everything from the transfer folder** | Imports every `.axeworld` file in the `world-transfer` folder |
| **Desktop app** | **Import**, then **Bring in your worlds from the web game…** | Opens one `.axeprofile` file |

The file pickers are not on Android, which cannot open a world file yet.

## Taking your browser worlds to the desktop app

Worlds you build in the browser live **in that browser**, on that computer. If you want to carry on in the free desktop app, you don't have to move them one at a time:

1. In the **browser**, open **Your worlds** and click **Save everything to a file**. Your browser downloads a single `.axeprofile` file with every world you have saved there, plus your Trials times and completed challenges. (No worlds yet? It still saves your Trials records, and says so.)
2. Open the **desktop app**, go to **Your worlds**, click **Import**, then **Bring in your worlds from the web game…**
3. Pick the file you just downloaded. Your worlds appear in the list and your Trials records are merged in. For each trial you keep whichever time is **faster**, and a challenge you've completed anywhere stays completed.

**Nothing is ever overwritten.** If a world coming in has the same name as one already on the desktop, the incoming copy is kept **alongside** it, named `<name> (web)`. You'll see a line saying how many worlds arrived and how many had to be renamed.

> This is entirely a file on your own computer: a download, then a file picker. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, nothing is sent to us, and you don't need to be signed in.

## What is not in the file

A file made to share, back up or move leaves out the private record of who placed each block. That record belongs to the world's owner and stays on their computer. See **[Who Built It](who-built-it.md)**.

## More to play

The lobby's **More to play** page holds the two official games, **Hash Dash** and **Satori Rush**. Press **Play** on a card and it launches into its own fresh arena. They are built into the game, so they work with no network at all. See **[Minigames](minigames.md)**.

On the **web** build the page also has **Look up a creator**. Paste someone's **npub** and press **Look up** to see the experiences and skins they have shared, and to try them on. The desktop app shows just the official games, and, below them, **More games**: Stampede, Twang Thang and Cluster Flock, three Android games from the same makers. Press **See it** on a card and your browser opens the More games page on axenstax.com, where the download is. (The web build has no More games section.)
