# Getting Started

This is your first five minutes in Axe'n'Stax. By the end of it, you'll have a tool, some food, and a sense of what to do next.

## Step 1 — Make a world

From the main menu, click **+ Create New World** (same button whether this is your first world or your tenth). Pick a name. Choose **Survival** (the default — health, hunger, durability all matter) or **Creative** (invulnerable, fly, instant break). For a first play, Survival is what makes the game feel like a game.

There's a **Spawn** dropdown above the world list. By default it's "Default (saved position)" — leave it alone for your first world. If you want to start next to a village from the very first second, pick **Near a village**.

There's also a **COMMANDS** toggle, **ON** by default. Leave it on — it's what lets you open the chat/command overlay (T or `/`) in that world later. If you switch it off, that world can never open chat or run commands, which means everything in **[Chat & Commands](chat-and-commands.md)** and **[Cheats & Op Commands](cheats.md)** is unavailable for it.

Click **Create & Play**.

## Step 2 — Look around

You'll wake up somewhere on the grass. **WASD** to walk, **mouse** to look. **Space** to jump. **Shift** to sneak. Press **F3** if you ever want to see your coordinates and biome — it's the debug overlay.

Take a moment to spot some trees. You're going to need wood.

## Step 3 — Get wood

Walk up to a tree. **Left-click and hold** on a log — you'll see it crack. After a few seconds the log breaks and you pick it up. Repeat until you have at least 3 logs.

## Step 4 — Make your first tools

Press **E** to open your inventory. You see your 9 hotbar slots at the bottom and a 2×2 crafting grid at the top right.

- **One log in any single slot** → click the result → you get 4 oak planks.
- **Two planks stacked vertically** → 4 sticks.
- **Three planks in a row across the top + two sticks in a column under the middle plank** → a wooden pickaxe.

Close the inventory (E again or Esc). Hold the pickaxe by selecting its hotbar slot (number key 1–9 or scroll wheel).

## Step 5 — Eat something

Hunger ticks down while you play. Find a cow, pig, sheep, or chicken — they wander on grass. Left-click them to attack. After a couple of swings they die and drop raw meat plus a few other things.

Press **1–9** to select the raw meat slot in your hotbar. **Right-click** to eat. Raw meat heals a little; cooked meat heals a lot. We'll get to cooking soon.

## Step 6 — Peek at what's in the game (B key)

Once you've got the basics, press **B** any time to open the **Inventory Explorer** — a browse-everything pane that shows every block, tool, and material in the game. Type "iron" or "cake" to filter. In Survival you'll see "×N" next to items you already have. In Creative you can click any entry to add one to your bag. **Esc** closes.

It's the fastest way to see what's possible before you go looking for it.

## Step 7 — Find a village (recommended)

Villages are where quests, trade, and reputation live. Walk in any direction — Plains and Forest biomes are the only places villages spawn, and there's roughly one per 32-chunk grid cell. You'll know you've found one when the **"You've found a village!"** toast pops up and a small reputation panel appears in the bottom-left of your screen.

Talk to a villager (**right-click** when within ~4 blocks). Accept a quest. The rest of this guide tells you what to do next.
