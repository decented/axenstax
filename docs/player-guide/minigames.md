# Minigames

Axe'n'Stax has two built-in **Experiences** — **Hash Dash** and **Satori Rush** —
run by the **Scenario** system. There are two ways to start one: type the
`/scenario` command in the in-game chat (**T** or **/**), or **▶ Play** the
**AxeNStax Official** entry in the lobby's **Stash Column** (which launches each
into its own fresh arena — see [Cloud Saves & the Stash Column](cloud-and-stash.md)).
This page is the quick **rules** reference — for a full *how to win* walkthrough,
follow the tutorial links.

## Hash Dash

> **Start:** `/scenario hash-dash`

A **3-minute** (3600-tick) mining sprint. You begin with an **empty inventory** —
completely bare-handed, same as Satori Rush — so the winning strategy is to
speed-craft your way up the tool tree inside the timer. Every block you break adds **"work"** to your
score, and **harder blocks are worth far more** (pure deepslate and stone are the
the highest scorers; leaves, sand and dirt are nearly worthless; bare-fist/instant breaks
score nothing). Highest total work when the clock hits zero **wins**. Hash Dash is
**transient** — it runs on your current world and does **not** overwrite your save.

→ **[How to Win Hash Dash](https://learn.axenstax.com/docs/journey/learn-journey/win-hash-dash.md)**

## Satori Rush

> **Start:** `/scenario satori-rush`

An **untimed** race from an **empty inventory** to your **first Satori** — the
world's **Genesis Block**. The clock counts **up** (lower is better) and only ticks
while you're playing; the world **saves**, so you can do it across several
sessions. Nothing is made easier — it's a full survival climb (wood → stone → iron
→ diamond pickaxe → deep to Y≤9 → mine fresh pure deepslate). When you get that
first Satori, your time is recorded and you can keep playing.

→ **[How to Win Satori Rush](https://learn.axenstax.com/docs/journey/learn-journey/win-satori-rush.md)**

## The Challenge Board (Trials)

Press **J** in-game to open the **Challenge Board**. It lists **Trials** — dozens
of small, authored objectives that nudge you to go try a specific game feature
you might not have found on your own ("plant and harvest a crop", "tame a wolf",
"wire up a light with a lever", "sell something at a Vendor Block", "build
something in the Workshop", "ride a cart down powered rail", and so on). There
are around **45 Trials** spanning nearly every system in the game — farming,
taming, combat, electricity, the vendor economy, the Workshop, rail riding, and
more. Unlike Hash Dash and Satori Rush, Trials aren't races against a clock or
each other — they're bite-sized "try this" prompts you can pick up and complete
at your own pace on your own world.
