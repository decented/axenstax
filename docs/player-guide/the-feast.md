# The Feast

A Feast is a meal you share. You put food on a **Feast Table**, everyone nearby gets a share, and the place around the table comes a little more alive.

## Make a Feast Table

Craft it at a **Crafting Table**. The shape is two rows of three, and it can sit anywhere on the grid:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **Plank** | **Plank** | **Plank** |
| **Stick** | **Bread** | **Stick** |

- Any three planks will do, from any wood.
- The loaf of **Bread** goes in the middle of the bottom row.
- In Creative mode the Feast Table is in the creative inventory too.

Place the table like any block. You can mine it with an axe and it drops itself.

## Hold a Feast

1. Pick up a **stack of food**. It must be at least **four** of the same thing, and food you can eat. Bread, cooked meat and vegetables all work.
2. Stand near the table and **right-click it** with the stack in your hand.

The game finds your **guests**:

- **Other players** within **48 blocks** of the table.
- **Villagers and peddlers** within **32 blocks**. Up to the nearest twelve count.
- You are not a guest. You are the host.

Then:

- The **whole stack** is shared out evenly. If it does not divide exactly, the nearest guests get the extra one each.
- Each player guest finds their share on the ground at their feet, as real items.
- A villager's share is used up on the spot. They eat it.
- The table turns into a **Feast Marker** and a burst of confetti plays.
- A line appears: **"The Feast of Long Hollow, 5 guests."** The name is made up from the place. The same spot always has the same name.

Right-click a Feast Marker any time to read its name and how many guests it fed. Break it and it drops a Feast Table again.

## What it does

- It gives the place around the table a lift in **[bloom](bloom.md)** that fades slowly over a while. A bigger feast lifts it more.
- Each Feast Marker also leaves a **small lasting lift** where it stands, so a place with a Feast Marker stays a little warmer. Several markers add up, up to a limit.

A Feast is about people together. Feasting does not make food, and it does not turn into anything else.

## Limits

| What you see | Why |
|---|---|
| "A feast needs guests. Bring friends, or find a village." | No one is in range. Nothing is used up. |
| "Hold something to share at the feast." | Your hand is empty. |
| "A feast needs at least four things to eat." | The stack is too small, or is not food. |
| "This place has feasted today." | Each 16 by 16 place can hold **one Feast per game day**. Try another place, or wait for tomorrow. |

- You hand over the **whole stack**, not a few items from it. Hold only what you want to give.
- In **Creative** mode your stack is not used up, because nothing is.
- It works in Survival and Creative, in single-player, on a LAN and online. On someone else's world the host's computer decides who counts as a guest.
- The Feast surge fades if you save and reload. The Feast Marker's small lasting lift stays.
