# Who Built It (Credit That Travels)

In Axe'n'Stax, **blocks are used up** and **ideas are not**. If you give a block away, it is gone from your hands. A Plan, an idea for how to build something, can be built again and again, and it keeps a line that says where it came from.

This page covers how the game keeps track of that: block credit, Plan credit lines and Worn Paths.

## Block credit

The world remembers **which player placed each block**.

- It works for **signed-in players** and for the **players sitting at the same computer** in split-screen.
- **Guests** who are not signed in are anonymous. So are natural blocks, and so is everything placed before this feature existed.
- A block that moves, pushed by a piston or fallen, becomes nobody's. So does a block that is broken, burnt or blown up.

That is all it is used for:

1. **[Commons](commons.md) protection.** Inside a Commons, nobody can break another player's block.
2. **Built together.** When more than one person has built in the same place, **[bloom](bloom.md)** gets a small lift.
3. **Plots passing on.** See Commons: a friend's Plot passes to the village if they stay away for 60 real days.

**What it is not:**

- It is **never shown per person.** No screen, message, toast or list names who built a block. The Commons refusal says "Someone else built this." and never says who.
- It is **kept on the computer that holds the world**, your own, or the host's or the server's. No names from it are ever sent to other players.
- It is **left out when you export a world** or save a copy to share. Who built what is the world owner's own record, and it does not travel with the file.

## Plan credit lines

A **Plan** is a captured build you can rebuild anywhere. See **[Build Schematics](build-schematics.md)**. Every Plan carries a credit line, and the **Architect's Plaque** that appears when a build finishes shows it.

The plaque reads like this:

- **"From a Plan by npub1abcdefg…, after Hut, Cabin"** means this Plan was made by that person, building on the Plans called Hut and Cabin.
- **"A local Plan"** means the Plan has no named author.
- **"Built 3 times in this world."** and **"2 more were built from it."** show how often the design has been used here. Plans that someone built on top of count towards the "built from it" line.

The counts are for the **whole world**. They never say who built it. A Plan with no builds yet shows no counts line at all.

**Ideas copy, and credit goes with them.** Building from a Plan does not use the Plan up, and the credit line stays attached, so the person who had the idea is not forgotten. Blocks and ideas are different things. A block you hand over is no longer yours, and an idea can be shared and still be yours.

## Worn Paths

Walk over the same grass often enough and it wears into a **Worn Path**.

- It takes about **24 footsteps** on the same patch of grass. Standing still, or jumping on the spot, does not count.
- A Worn Path looks like bare dirt and breaks like dirt. It does not grow back into grass.
- The first time you stand on one, Satoshi says: "A path! Feet wore that in. Places people use get paths."
- Worn Paths **do not count against a place** in bloom. A road is a sign people use a place. It is not damage.
- Worn Paths only form in **Survival**. A Creative lawn round a build will not wear away.

Half-worn grass is not saved. Only the finished path is.

## Lived in

The world also notes which places people come back to and sleep in, as a count of days per place. It holds no names. It is why a place people use over many days can bloom a little more. It is kept when you export a world, because it holds no identities.
