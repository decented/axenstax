# Tips & Tricks

Small things that make a big difference. Skim this any time, but especially after you've played for a few hours.

## First-five-minutes kit

The fastest path to "I have everything I need":

1. Punch a tree → 4 planks → 4 sticks → wooden pickaxe.
2. Mine stone (any cobble blocks) → 4 cobble → stone pickaxe + stone sword.
3. Find a cow / pig / sheep → 2 raw meat → eat it.
4. Place a Workbench.
5. Walk to the nearest village (or use **Near a village** spawn). Accept your first quest.

You can do all of this in about 4 minutes of play.

## Inventory hygiene

- Tools you use constantly belong in **slots 1–3**.
- Food belongs in **slot 4**.
- Building blocks in **slots 5–9**.
- Q-drop junk you don't need.
- Press **B** to browse every item in the game (search + filter). In Survival, hover shows you how many of each you currently hold — quick "do I have any bone meal?" check without scrolling.

A tidy hotbar saves a surprising amount of friction.

## Sword discipline

- A **wooden sword** kills any single weak hostile (a lone Brigand or Hyena) if you fight smart.
- An **iron sword** is your first "I can take on a Bear or a Marauder" weapon.
- **Jump-attack** for criticals — costs nothing, +50% damage.
- **Don't get surrounded by a Hyena pack.** Put your back to a wall, or bow them from range.

## Stay above 18 hunger

You only regenerate health when hunger ≥ 18. Below that, you don't heal — fighting becomes a one-way trip downward.

Eat **before** you fight, not after.

## Watch the durability bar

The toast warns you at 10%. By the time you see it, you have maybe 5 more swings. **Carry a backup tool** — durability surprises are the #1 cause of "I died in a cave to a half-HP Bear".

## Mining safely

- Always carry **at least 8 torches** when going deep. Light up everything behind you.
- Mine **diagonally upward**, never straight up — a falling sand or gravel block can suffocate you.
- Mine **diagonally downward**, not straight down — you can't see what's below your feet.
- When you find a **lava pool**, back off. Lava + falling block = instant death.
- If you stumble into a **pack of Hyenas** in a cave, back up to a corridor so they can't surround you.

## Build a sleeping spot near every cave

A 3×3 cobble box with a **bed** in the back corner keeps you safe overnight. Put one near every interesting cave entrance — when night falls you're 10 seconds from safety, and right-clicking the bed at night skips to morning + restores full health + sets your respawn point. See **[Crafting](crafting.md)** for the bed recipe (3 wool + 3 planks).

## Village reputation strategy

- **Carpenter quests are cheap and easy.** 16 sticks for 10 sats + 5 rep. Knock out 3 of these and you're at +15 (Friendly tier).
- **Kill quests pay more rep + sats than Fetch.** Trade time-cost for higher reward.
- **Don't decline a quest you'll regret accepting.** 5-minute cooldown per villager.
- **Stay above Neutral with your home village.** Once you're Friendly + Beloved, quest rewards scale up with reputation tier.

## Use the campfire for free pest control

A lit campfire **draws hostile mobs toward it** (within ~12 blocks). They stop at the heat radius. If you're near a village, the Knight does all the work.

A village campfire + a Knight = the safest place to be at night.

## Q-drop = give

In split-screen multiplayer, **Q-drop is how you give items to your partner**. Drop, they pick up — instantly for them (1.5-second delay for you). Use it to share food, tools, or to dump excess.

## Reputation decay

Reputation slowly drifts toward zero — one point per in-game day. If you go away for a while and come back, your standing won't have collapsed, but it won't be where you left it either. Visit your favourite village every few days to keep it Beloved.

## The spawn dropdown is your friend

If you wandered far from your home village before saving, set the dropdown to **Near a village** before re-loading. Saves a long trek.

## Survival cheat: `/give` for first kit

If you're stuck and just want to make progress, `/give iron_pickaxe` + `/give iron_sword` + `/give bread 16` gets you on your feet. The cheats flag goes up on your world, so survival-purist achievements (when they ship) won't apply — but you can still play normally afterward.

For a "no shame" run: `/gamemode creative` for 30 seconds to build a starting house, switch back to `survival`. The world remembers, but the game still works the same way.

## Find the gossip

Every villager has a gossip line below their quest in the dialogue. It refreshes once per in-game day. **Read them.** They sometimes contain practical hints — "the well water is sweeter on the east side", "if you find a Workbench in the wild, treat it kind". The future plan is for gossip to also tip you off about other player vendors + incoming raids.

## When in doubt, F3 + Esc

- **F3** shows you coordinates, biome, performance, current targeted block. Use it to figure out "where am I?" or "what's that block called?".
- **Esc** is the universal back button. Close menu / close dialogue / open pause.

## Graphics — Esc → Graphics

The **Graphics** panel (pause menu → **Graphics**) is where you trade prettiness
for frame rate. Pick a preset (Potato / Low / Medium / High / Ultra) or turn the
individual dials — everything applies straight away, no restart, and it's
remembered on this device.

| Dial | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Render distance** | How far you can see (4–16 chunks). The biggest single cost. |
| **Render scale** | Draws the world smaller then stretches it up. The rescue dial on a slow laptop. |
| **Frame limit** | VSync / capped / uncapped. |
| **Field of view** + **Sensitivity** | Personal comfort — these never change your preset. |
| **Brightness** | "It's too dark" lives here. |
| **Fog** | Distance haze. |
| **Particles** | Off / Reduced / Full. |
| **Mipmaps** | Smooths *distant* textures so far-off ground and walls stop shimmering as you walk. Blocks right next to you stay pixel-sharp. **Off by default** — turn it on, look at the horizon, and keep whichever you prefer. |

## Have fun

This is the most important tip. The game's a sandbox + a world to explore + a small economy. There's no "right way" to play. Build, mine, farm, quest, fight, talk to villagers. Whatever's fun for you that day.
