# Blight (Why One Crop Is Not Enough)

A big field of just one crop is fragile. Pests and sickness love it. In Axe'n'Stax that is called **blight**, and it is the game's way of telling you that a mixed place is a healthy place.

## What triggers it

Blight can only start in a **big, single-crop field**. All of these have to be true for one 16 by 16 place:

- It holds **at least 64 crops**.
- **Nine in every ten** of them, or more, are the **same kind**.

Mature crops count towards the 64. Wheat, carrots, potatoes, corn, papyrus, cotton, hemp and farmed cornflowers, poppies and buttercups all count, each as its own kind. Wild flowers in a meadow do not. Flowers only count when they are growing on tilled soil.

**A small field never blights.** A first wheat patch is nowhere near 64 plants. A field with two or three kinds in it never blights either.

## What it looks like

A single plant in the field turns into a **Blighted Crop**: a small, stunted, straw-coloured plant that looks wrong beside its healthy neighbours.

- Each growing crop in a one-crop field has a small chance of blighting each growth step. About one in forty.
- Once a Blighted Crop is there, it **spreads to the same kind of crop right next to it**, about one in eight per neighbour each growth step. That happens wherever it is, even if the field is mixed by then.
- It only ever spreads to the **same** crop. A different crop beside it is safe.

The first time you come near one, Satoshi says: "That crop's gone grey. A field of just one plant gets sick easily. Try mixing in other plants."

## What a Blighted Crop does

- It never grows again.
- It **drops nothing** when you break it.
- Bone meal and fertiliser do nothing to it. Nothing is used up if you try.
- In **[bloom](bloom.md)** it counts as bare ground, like stripped stone.

## What to do about it

1. **Break the Blighted Crops.** Replant the spot.
2. **Mix your crops.** Plant two or three kinds in the same place. Break a big field up with a different crop, or split it into smaller fields.
3. **Add other life.** Flowers, a few trees, water and animals all help the place, and bloom likes variety.

Breaking a Blighted Crop removes it. Blight that is already standing in a world stays until you break it, even if blight is switched off by Creative mode or a Trial. Those only stop new blight starting.

## Limits

- Blight is **off in Creative mode**.
- Blight is **off inside Trials**, so a farming Trial never goes wrong because of it.
- It works on any world, in single-player, on a LAN and on a dedicated server.
- A field of just over 64 can fall under the line after its first blight and stop starting new ones. Blight that is already there still spreads.
