# Dyes, Fibre & Magnesium

Three new material lines landed together (2026-05-27): **colour** (flowers → dyes → wallpaper), **fibre** (cotton & hemp → string & rope), and **magnesium** (a mineral with three jobs). All of it works in the current build.

> Make a **new world** to see the new plants and ore — existing saves keep their already-generated terrain.

## Colour — flowers, dyes & wallpaper

### Flowers
Three flowers grow on grassland — the game's first flowers:

- **Cornflower** (blue), **Field Poppy** (red), **Buttercup** (yellow).

Point your crosshair at one and **break it** to collect the flower **plus a seed or two**. Plant those seeds on **tilled soil** (just like a crop) and you can grow your own flower patch — each grows through three stages, then drops the flower and more seeds, so you never run out. Each flower presses into its **primary dye** (one flower → one dye).

### Mixing — the paintbox
You don't need a flower for every colour. Mix what you've got:

| You mix… | …you get |
|---|---|
| Red + Yellow *(or Poppy + Buttercup)* | **Orange** |
| Yellow + Blue *(or Buttercup + Cornflower)* | **Green** |
| Blue + Red *(or Cornflower + Poppy)* | **Purple** |
| Red + Yellow + Blue *(or all three flowers)* | **Brown** |
| Green + Blue | **Cyan** |
| Purple + Pink *(or Red + Blue + White)* | **Magenta** |
| **White** tints (three primaries only) | Red+White → **Pink**, Green+White → **Lime**, Blue+White → **Light Blue**. Other colours (Orange, Purple, Yellow, Brown, Cyan, Magenta) don't have a White-tint recipe. |
| White + **Black** | **Grey** (Grey + White → **Light Grey**) |

For four of the secondaries — Orange, Green, Purple and Brown — you've got two paths: mix the **dyes**, or mix the two **flowers** directly, same result. The rest are dye-only. Mixing two dyes gives you **two** of the new colour, so you never run dry. That's **16 colours** in all, from just five base dyes.

**Black** and **White** don't come from flowers:
- **Black Dye** ← an **Ink Sac** (dropped by **squid** in the ocean).
- **White Dye** ← **Bone Meal**.

### Wallpaper
The simplest dye use: **Papyrus Sheet + any dye → 3 coloured Wallpaper blocks.** All **16 colours**, one per dye — place them to decorate your walls.

### Dyed décor
Once you've got dyes, you can brighten your base with six more decorations — most come in all 16 colours, but not all of them take dye:

- **Bunting** — `Dye + String + Dye` in a row makes **4** strings of little triangular flags. Great for parties and village squares. All 16 colours.
- **Paper Lantern** — `Papyrus + Stick + Dye` in a row makes a glowing paper cube. **It gives off light** (a soft glow, a touch dimmer than a torch), so it lights a room *and* looks pretty. All 16 colours.
- **Kite** — `Cloth + String + Dye` in a row makes a colourful kite to hang up. All 16 colours.
- **Banner** — a tall pole with a dyed flag on top: stack `Dye / Cloth / Stick` in a column. Your first use for **Cloth** (4 Cotton makes 1 Cloth). All 16 colours.
- **Sail** — like a banner but woven from canvas: stack `Dye / Canvas / Stick` in a column. Your first use for **Canvas** (4 Hemp Fibre makes 1 Canvas). All 16 colours.
- **Tent** — a little canvas dome: canvas across the top, a stick stake in each bottom corner. ⚠️ **No dye slot** — it's a single undyed block, not a 16-colour item. For now it's just decorative — bigger tents and sleeping in them are coming later.

## Fibre — cotton, hemp, string & rope

A **fibre triangle**: each fibre has its own job.

- **Cotton** — grows wild in **savanna**. Soft fibre → **String** (for bows, fishing rods).
- **Hemp** — grows wild in **plains/forest**. Strong fibre → **Rope**.
- **Wool** — from sheep, unchanged.

### Farming it
Break a **wild** cotton or hemp plant to get its **seeds**. Plant the seeds on **tilled soil** — they grow through four stages, and the mature crop drops **fibre + more seeds**, so you're self-sustaining. (The wild plants give seeds; the fibre itself comes from harvesting your crop.)

- **Cotton → String:** 1 Cotton → 2 String.
- **Hemp Fibre → Rope:** stack **3 Hemp Fibre** in a vertical line → 1 Rope. Rope is heavier cordage than string, so it costs more fibre.

> String used to come from sheep wool — that was a stopgap. Cotton is its proper home now; wool goes back to being just wool.

### Rope, Leads & cloth
Rope is the start of a whole line of gear:

- **Lead** = Rope on top, then String, then String (a vertical line) → 1 Lead. Use it to walk an animal around (see **[Combat & Mobs](combat-and-mobs.md)**).
- **Cloth** = 4 Cotton in a 2×2 square → 1 Cloth (soft fabric — for banners and kites).
- **Canvas** = 4 Hemp Fibre in a 2×2 square → 1 Canvas (tough sailcloth — for sails and tents).

## Magnesium — one mineral, three jobs

**Magnesium Ore** is a new ore you mine mid-depth (a **stone pickaxe** or better) — it generates at Y 30-48 with no biome restriction, so any stone at that depth has a shot at it. (Desert and mountain rock is where you'll find **Rock Salt** instead — a different mineral, easy to mix up with Magnesium.) It drops **Magnesium**, which makes three things:

- **🌱 Fertiliser** = `Magnesium + Sulphur`. Right-click a growing crop with it to **jump it forward a stage** — magnesium is the element at the heart of chlorophyll, so this is real plant food. Works on every crop, including the new cotton & hemp.
- **🔥 Magnesium Firestarter** = `Magnesium + Iron Ingot`. Lights campfires like flint-and-steel, and it's **reusable**.
- **✨ Sparkler** (`Magnesium + Stick`) and **Flare** (`Magnesium + Papyrus`) — bright magnesium pyrotechnics. (Their sparkle/light effects are still being polished.)

## Blueprints are blue now

Captured **Plans** develop their blueprint-blue out in the sun — lay a captured print outside in daylight and watch it deepen through four stages. See **[Build Schematics](build-schematics.md)** for the full cyanotype process.
