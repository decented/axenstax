# The Grey Quarry

The Grey Quarry is a Trial about a place, not a person. You start in a stripped stone quarry that has lost almost everything alive, and your job is to bring it back.

> Somebody took everything this place had and walked away. It's still here, though. Let's see if it remembers how to be alive.

## How to find it

Press **J** to open the Challenge Board, the list of **Trials**. See **[Minigames](minigames.md)**. The Grey Quarry sits on the shelf labelled **Bloom**.

It is not on the list at first. It appears the first time a place in one of your worlds goes **grey** (a stripped patch left bare for a good while), or once you have finished the **Power Plant** Trial.

## The start

You land in a fresh quarry world, with a survival kit.

**The quarry** is about 24 blocks by 24. It is a stone quarry in four terraces, with a few rows of bare dirt and three oak stumps. A spring, a water source held back by two cobblestone blocks, sits at the top. Take the stones away and the water runs down the old stream bed.

**Beside it** is a fenced meadow with **2 sheep, 1 cow and 2 rabbits**. A chest sits near where you start.

**In your kit**

| Item | How many |
|---|---|
| Oak saplings | 4 |
| Birch saplings | 4 |
| Spruce saplings | 4 |
| Wheat seeds | 8 |
| Carrots | 8 |
| Potatoes | 8 |
| Bone meal | 8 |
| Bread | 16 |
| Wheat | 8 |
| Leads | 3 |
| Bucket, stone shovel and wooden hoe | 1 each |
| Feast Table | 1 |

## The goal

Bring the quarry to **Alive**, and keep it there for **20 seconds**.

The task reads **"Bring the quarry back to life"**, and under it you see the stage words: **Grey and still**, **Something's stirring**, **Waking up**, **Alive**. There is never a number. See **[How Places Come Alive](bloom.md)**.

## How to do it

Everything alive helps. A few things to try:

- **Open the spring.** Water brings life, and water helps the whole place.
- **Plant different kinds of tree.** Oak, birch and spruce all count separately.
- **Plant crops.** Use the hoe on the dirt rows and sow wheat, carrots and potatoes.
- **Bring animals in.** Use a Lead to walk the sheep, the cow and the rabbits in from the meadow.

Water, animals and crops get the quarry a good way, but not all the way. **Trees are what carry it over the line**, and saplings take a while to grow. Plant them first.

A Feast Table is in the kit, but a Feast needs guests, and there are no villagers in the quarry. A Feast is for your own worlds, where there is someone to feast with. See **[The Feast](the-feast.md)**.

## What is different here

- **The quarry heals much faster than a normal world.** That is a setting for this Trial only. Nothing else in the game speeds up.
- The day is held at daytime and there are no hostile mobs.
- It is a fresh arena world made for this Trial. It does not change your own worlds.

## The end

When the quarry is Alive, you see an end card with a **four-petal flower**. The petals are **life, ideas, people and time**.

- The **life** and **people** petals open, and are as big as each one helped.
- The **ideas** and **time** petals are closed buds for now.

Under the flower is a single sentence about how you did it, for example **"You brought it back mostly through life."** It is never a score. Satoshi says: "Look at it. Nobody owns that colour. It's just there now, for whoever comes next."

Your Trial record notes **how** you did it, so the card can show the ways you have brought it back. You can press **Try again** and the quarry is reset, grey again, to try a different way.
