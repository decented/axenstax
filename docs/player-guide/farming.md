# Farming

You can grow your own food and your own materials. A patch of tilled soil, some seeds and a little patience gives you a food supply that never runs out — no more chasing cows.

This page explains **how growing works** and **what you can grow today**. If you want a step-by-step walkthrough, head to **[Start your first farm](https://learn.axenstax.com/docs/journey/learn-journey/your-first-farm.md)**.

## How crops grow

Field crops need **Tilled Soil** to grow in.

1. Use a **Hoe** on the **top face** of **Dirt** or **Grass**. It turns into **Tilled Soil** and uses up 1 point of hoe durability.
2. **Right-click** the tilled soil with a **seed** to plant it.
3. The crop grows in the air block **above** the soil, through 4 stages (0 → 3). **Stage 3** is mature and ready to harvest.

Crops do **not** re-seed themselves. When you harvest, the soil goes back to plain Tilled Soil and you replant from the seeds you collected.

### Light

A crop only grows when there's enough light. It needs an **effective light level of 9 or more**.

| Where the crop is | Light | Grows? |
|---|---|---|
| Under open sky | Effective light is always **11** (skylight is 15 in open sky, day or night — a night-floor offset of 4 is subtracted to get the effective value; only the on-screen rendering dims at night) | ✅ Grows day and night |
| Underground or under a roof | No skylight | ⚠️ Needs a **Torch** nearby |

If a crop is too dark it just sits there at its current stage. Add light and it carries on.

### Growth speed

Each stage takes **200 ticks (about 10 seconds)** by default.

Plant within **4 blocks of Water** and growth is **twice as fast** — 100 ticks (about 5 seconds) per stage. The water can even be one row **below** the soil, so you can run irrigation channels under your field.

**Rain waters every outdoor crop for free** — while it's raining, every crop under open sky grows at the same doubled speed as if it had water nearby, no irrigation needed. A crop under a roof doesn't get this (it's not getting rained on).

### Fertiliser shortcut

Right-click a growing crop with **Fertiliser** to push it forward **one whole stage** instantly. Fertiliser is made from **Magnesium + Sulphur** — see **[Dyes, Fibre & Magnesium](dyes-fibre-and-magnesium.md)**.

There's a stronger accelerant too: **Bonemeal** uses the same right-click slot but pushes a crop forward **1-2 stages** at once, instead of Fertiliser's flat one stage.

## Harvesting

**Break the mature crop block** to harvest it. There's no right-click harvest in the live game — you have to break it.

A mature crop drops its food (and usually some seeds). The soil resets to **Tilled Soil**, ready to replant.

## Live crops

These are the crops you can plant and grow right now.

| Crop | How to grow | Drops when mature |
|---|---|---|
| **Wheat** | Plant **Wheat Seeds** | 1 **Wheat** + 1–3 **Wheat Seeds** |
| **Carrot** | Plant a **Carrot** (the carrot is both seed and food) | 1–4 **Carrots** |
| **Potato** | Plant a **Potato** (the potato is both seed and food) | 1–4 **Potatoes** |
| **Corn** | Plant **Corn Seeds** | 1 **Corn** + 1–3 **Corn Seeds** |

What you can make:

- **Wheat** → **Bread** (raw wheat isn't edible). 9 wheat → **Hay Bale**.
- **Carrot** → eat raw, or bake into **Baked Carrot**.
- **Potato** → bake into **Baked Potato** or **Loaded Baked Potato**.
- **Corn** → eat raw, or bake into **Baked Corn** ("corn on the cob").

## Fibre crops

Cotton and hemp give you materials rather than food. The full chains are on **[Dyes, Fibre & Magnesium](dyes-fibre-and-magnesium.md)**.

| Crop | How to start | How to grow | Drops when mature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cotton** | Break a wild **Cotton** plant in **Savanna** for **Cotton Seeds** | Plant **Cotton Seeds** | 1 **Cotton** + 1–2 **Cotton Seeds** |
| **Hemp** | Break a wild **Hemp** plant in **Plains** or **Forest** for **Hemp Seeds** | Plant **Hemp Seeds** | 1 **Hemp Fibre** + 1–2 **Hemp Seeds** |

- 1 **Cotton** → 2 **String**. 4 **Cotton** → **Cloth**.
- 3 **Hemp Fibre** → **Rope**. 4 **Hemp Fibre** → **Canvas**.

## Dye flowers

There are three flower types in the game: **Cornflower**, **Field Poppy** and **Buttercup**. They grow wild on grass, and you can also farm them from their seeds (**Cornflower Seeds** and so on). A mature flower drops the flower block + 1–2 seeds.

| Flower | Dye |
|---|---|
| **Cornflower** | **Blue Dye** |
| **Field Poppy** | **Red Dye** |
| **Buttercup** | **Yellow Dye** |

The full dye tree is on **[Dyes, Fibre & Magnesium](dyes-fibre-and-magnesium.md)**.

## Papyrus Reed

**Papyrus Reed** is a water-side reed, not a field crop — **no tilling needed**. Plant a **Papyrus Reed** on **Dirt**, **Grass** or **Sand** right next to water. It **auto-regrows** like sugarcane: harvesting drops 1–2 reeds and the root keeps growing, so you never have to replant.

It also grows wild along lake and ocean shorelines. Reeds become **Papyrus Sheet** — the paper and blueprint chain.

## Berry Bush (wild only)

**Berry Bushes** appear rarely on grass. Break a mature bush for 1 **Berries**. Berries feed you and are used to tame **Nostriches** and **Foxes** — see **[Nostrich](nostrich.md)** and **[Combat & Mobs](combat-and-mobs.md)**.

> **Coming soon:** planting and regrowing berry bushes. For now a wild bush is a one-shot — break it and it's gone.

## Tree saplings

**Tree saplings** are live — plant one on **Dirt** or **Grass** and it grows into a full tree over time, matching its species. See **[Blocks and mining](blocks-and-mining.md)** for the block-and-tool details.
