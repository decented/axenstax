# The Tally (Grown-Ups' Panel)

The Tally is a small panel for grown-ups. It shows what has been done in a world, and how alive the world's places are. It is **off by default**.

> **The Tally counts what was taken and made. Bloom is how alive the places are.**

Bloom is never a score for a player. What a child sees is the world itself: its colour, its sound and its life. See **[How Places Come Alive](bloom.md)**. The Tally is the grown-up's view of the same thing.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings** and tick **Grown-up panel (F3)**.
2. In the world, press **F3**. The panel draws beside the debug overlay.

It is a personal setting on that computer. Quality presets never change it. The sats gauge keeps its own switch and is separate.

## What it shows

**The Tally**, for this world in this session:

| Row | What it counts |
|---|---|
| Blocks mined | Blocks broken |
| Blocks placed | Blocks put down |
| Items crafted | Things made at a crafting grid |
| Items smelted | Things collected from a furnace |
| Vendor trades | Completed trades at Vendor Blocks |
| Stock in containers | Items currently in chests and in dispensers or droppers in the parts of the world that are loaded |

**World bloom**, for the whole world:

| Row | What it is |
|---|---|
| Bloom, mean level | How alive the places are, on average |
| Life | Plants, trees, water and animals |
| Ideas | Plan designs built here, and builds made from them |
| People together | Players together, feasts, Commons, and things built together |
| Time | Places lived in over many days |
| Places measured | How many places the figures come from |

## What it is not

- **It is about the world, never a person.** There are no names and no per-child figures.
- **It saves nothing.** The counts belong to this session of this world. They start again when you change world or restart the game.
- **It does not set anything.** It is only a view.

## Limits

- On a joined client the panel shows a dash for Ideas and Time, because the host keeps those.
- The counts are for the actions this computer sees. On a hosted world, a friend's crafting and trades are not counted on the host's panel.
- A dedicated server keeps the same kind of figures, but counts only the block edits it accepts from players. Crafts, smelts and trades read zero there. The operator's web console does not show them yet.
- Auction sales are not counted, only Vendor Block trades.
- Nothing in the Tally is sent to AxeNStax.
