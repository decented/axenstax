# Your First Blocks

*The whole game starts with a single swing. Let's take it together.*

Everything around you is made of blocks — the grass, the trees, the hills, the
ground under your feet — and all of it is yours to take apart and rebuild. Here's
how it works.

## Break your first block

Look at something close by — a patch of grass, some dirt, the trunk of a tree.
Anything within arm's reach (about five blocks away).

Now **hold down the left mouse button.**

Watch it crack. Keep holding… and — *crunch* — it breaks, and the piece jumps
straight into your bag. That's mining! Let go, look at the next block, and do it
again. Mine a few. Feels good, doesn't it?

## You're already carrying it

Good news — you don't have to stop and pick anything up. Broken blocks come to you
on their own when you're close by.

Want to see what you've gathered? Press **E** to open your bag. There it all is.
Press **E** again to close it.

## Now put one back

Building is just mining in reverse.

1. Find your block in the **hotbar** — the row of slots along the bottom — and
   choose its slot with the **number keys** or the **scroll wheel.**
2. Point at where you'd like it to go: the ground, a wall, on top of another block.
3. **Right-click.**

There it is — you've placed a block.

Try this: stack two blocks up into a little tower, then knock it back down.
**Break, carry, place.** Once that loop feels natural, you can build absolutely
anything — it really is the heart of the whole game.

## A little secret: the right tool is *much* faster

Your bare hands work fine, but they're slow — and some blocks won't give you
anything at all unless you use the proper tool:

- A **pickaxe** for stone and ores
- An **axe** for logs and planks
- A **shovel** for dirt, sand, gravel and snow

Match the tool to the block and it breaks in a flash. (Stone and ores are a little
fussy — they need a strong enough pickaxe, or they'll crumble away to nothing. The
Wiki lists exactly which one you need, whenever you're curious.)

## 🎉 Look at you go

You can mine, you can carry, you can build — the three most important things in the
whole game, and you did them on your very first try.

You're doing it all with your bare hands for now, though. Let's fix that: next, you
'll build a workbench and make some proper tools.
