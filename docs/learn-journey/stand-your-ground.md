# Stand Your Ground

*Sooner or later something comes at you — a Brigand in the dark, a Bear in the woods. Running works, but sometimes you have to turn and fight. Here's how to win.*

## Make a weapon

Anything beats your bare fists (which barely scratch). At your **crafting table**:

- A **sword** is your best fighter — two of a material stacked above a single stick. A **stone sword** is a fine start; an **iron sword** (once you've smelted some iron) is better still.
- No sword to hand? Your **axe** actually hits *harder* than a sword of the same material — a brilliant backup weapon in a pinch.

Keep your weapon selected in your hotbar.

## How to fight

1. **Point at the mob** and **left-click** to swing. You reach about **three blocks.**
2. **Let each swing land before clicking again** — there's a short cooldown, so frantic clicking doesn't help.
3. Every hit **knocks the mob back** a little. **Sprint into your swing** for a bigger shove — handy for keeping a charging Brigand at arm's length.

### The big trick: jump-hits

Swing **while you're falling** — jump, then hit on the way down — and it's a **critical hit**, about **one-and-a-half times** the damage. Jump in, crit, step back. It's the difference-maker in a tough scrap.

## Stay alive

- You have **ten hearts.** Keep half an eye on them.
- **Keep your hunger up.** When your hunger bar is nearly full, your hearts slowly **heal on their own.** Let it run empty and the healing stops (and then the hurting starts). Carry cooked food and eat between fights.
- **Pick your ground.** Put your **back to a wall** so a pack of **Hyenas** can't get all the way around you, and thin them out one at a time.
- **Bears hit hard and take a while to drop** — keep your distance and lean on knockback, or pepper them with a **bow and arrows** from range if you've made some.

## A word on armour

Proper **armour** — helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots — soaks up a chunk of every hit, and you can craft and wear it right now. Shape leather, iron, diamond, or Satori at your **crafting table** (the recipe book shows you each piece's pattern), then open your inventory, **click** a piece to pick it up, and **click its armour slot** to put it on. The more you've got equipped, the less damage gets through.

## 🎉 You did it

You can handle yourself now. Most things out there would honestly rather not tangle with someone who's mastered the jump-hit.
