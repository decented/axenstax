# Smelt Iron in a Furnace

*Stone tools are good. Iron tools are better — tougher, faster, and able to dig things stone can't touch. To get iron you don't craft it, you melt it. That's what a furnace is for.*

(Quick note: the furnace is **not** the crafting table, and it's **not** the campfire. The campfire cooks food; the furnace melts ore into metal.)

## Build a furnace

First you need **cobblestone** — the rough grey blocks you get from mining ordinary **stone** with a pickaxe. Gather **eight** of them.

At your **crafting table**, lay them in a ring with the middle left empty:

```
[C][C][C]
[C][ ][C]
[C][C][C]
C = Cobblestone
```

That makes a **Furnace.** **Right-click** to place it down.

## Find and mine iron

Iron hides underground as **Iron Ore** — speckly stone you'll spot in caves or by digging down a little.

**Important:** you need at least a **stone pickaxe** to mine iron ore. A wooden one won't break it — so bring your stone pickaxe. Mine a few lumps of **Raw Iron.**

## Smelt it

**Right-click the furnace** to open it. There are **three** slots:

| Slot | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Top — Input** | your **Raw Iron** |
| **Bottom — Fuel** | something that burns: **coal** is best, but logs or planks work too |
| **Output** | the finished **Iron Ingot** appears here |

Put iron in the top and fuel in the bottom, and the furnace **lights itself** — no match needed. Each lump takes about **ten seconds.** One lump of **coal** smelts loads in a row (around two dozen), so it's well worth getting some.

Take your shiny **Iron Ingots** out of the output slot.

## Make iron tools

Back at your **crafting table**, use the ingots just like you used wood and stone — an **iron pickaxe**, **iron axe**, or **iron sword.** They last far longer, work faster, and the iron pickaxe is your ticket to the deeper, better stuff later on.

## 🎉 You did it

You've turned rock into metal. Iron gear is the real turning point — from "just surviving" to "properly kitted out."
