# Save Your First Build

*Made something you love? You can save it onto a blueprint and rebuild it any time, anywhere. Let's try a tiny one.*

A **schematic** is a saved copy of a build — capture it once, rebuild it whenever
you like. We'll do something small for your first go.

## Build something tiny

Make a little structure on flat ground — a 3×3 box with walls, or a small tower.
Keep it tiny for now.

## Get some Blueprint Paper

Craft **Blueprint Paper** (a **Papyrus Sheet**, an **Iron Ingot**, and **Salt**,
stacked in a column). One craft gives you a few sheets. You also need a
**Drafting Stamp** — the tool that does the capturing (craft one, or grab it in
Creative).

## Lay the paper and capture

1. **Place Blueprint Paper flat on the ground** all around the base of your build
   (solid ground under each sheet, open air above it).
2. Hold your **Drafting Stamp** and **right-click your build** (any block of it —
   the Stamp looks down to find the paper). The Stamp isn't used up. *(An empty
   hand won't do it — you need the Stamp.)*
3. A little dialog opens — give your build a **name**, pick a **licence**, and confirm.

The paper's used up, and a **plan** lands in your bag. The capture is exact —
**1:1** — and it even works underground; no sunlight needed to capture.

## Develop it in the sun

> **Playing in Creative?** Skip this whole step — your plan is **ready the
> instant you capture it**. Jump straight to "Rebuild it" below. (Developing in
> the sun is only for Survival.)

Your fresh plan is **undeveloped.** To finish it off:

1. Hold the plan and **right-click the ground** under **open sky** to lay down a
   **Latent Print.**
2. Leave it in **daylight** — about **90 seconds of direct sun.** A roof, trees, or
   night will pause it (don't worry, the progress is saved, it just waits).
3. When the plan's icon turns full **blue**, it's ready. **Mine the floor tile**
   it's attached to (break it, same as any block) to pick the plan back up.

## Rebuild it

1. Hold the **developed** plan and **right-click** → **Place in world.**
2. A see-through preview appears. Aim it where you want, and press **Q / E** to
   rotate. Green means good, red means something's in the way.
3. **Left-click** to confirm the spot, then pick how you want it built:
   - **Build it automatically** — sit back and watch it go up, block by block.
   - **Guide me — block by block** or **layer by layer** — you place every
     block yourself, with a glowing marker showing exactly what goes where next
     (handy if you'd rather build it with your own hands, or just watch how it
     goes together).

In **Survival**, an automatic build uses up the matching blocks from your bag as
it goes — and if you're short on materials, the game skips straight to the
layer-by-layer guide instead of failing, so you can see exactly what to go and
gather next. In **Creative**, every option is free. Either way it comes out the
**same size** you captured. Choose **Build it automatically** and, once it's
done, an **Architect Plaque** appears in the middle, showing who designed it —
that's you!

## 🎉 You did it

You captured a build, developed it, and rebuilt it. Now you can copy your best
creations anywhere — and if you quit halfway through a rebuild, it picks up right
where it left off when you come back.
