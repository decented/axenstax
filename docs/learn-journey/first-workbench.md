# Make Your First Workbench

*Almost everything good — tools, a bed, a furnace — needs a proper crafting table. So that's the very first thing we'll build.*

The little **2×2** grid in your bag can only make simple bits and pieces. The good
stuff needs a bigger **3×3** grid, and you get that from a **Workbench.**
Let's make one.

## First, get some wood

Walk up to a tree and **hold the left mouse button** on a log until it breaks. Grab
**three or four logs** while you're there.

## Turn logs into planks

Press **E** to open your bag. In the little 2×2 grid:

1. Pop **one log** into any cell.
2. Click the result — out come **four planks.**

Do that with a couple of logs so you've got planks to spare.

## Planks into a Workbench

Still in that 2×2 grid, fill **all four cells with planks:**

```
[P][P]
[P][P]
P = Planks
```

Click the result and you've got a **Workbench.**

## Put it down and open it up

1. Close your bag (**E** or **Esc**).
2. Select the Workbench in your hotbar.
3. Aim at the ground and **right-click** to place it.
4. Now **right-click the Workbench** — and there's your full **3×3 grid.**

## While you're here, make some sticks

You'll want sticks for nearly every tool. In either grid, stack **two planks, one
above the other:**

```
[P]
[P]
P = Planks  →  4 Sticks
```

## 🎉 You did it

A Workbench down, a 3×3 grid open — this is the doorway to pretty much
everything you'll ever make.
