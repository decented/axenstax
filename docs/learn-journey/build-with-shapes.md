# Build with Shapes

*Up to now everything's been a full cube — solid, square, one block. Lovely for
walls, but a house needs more than walls. Let's meet the **shaped blocks**: slabs
for steps, stairs you can walk up, and a door that actually opens. They're what
turn a box into somewhere you'd want to live.*

The one thing to hold onto: with these, **where you click matters**. A full cube
goes down the same way every time. A shaped block remembers which way it faces —
so you aim it by *where* and *how* you place it. You'll get the feel in a minute.

## Grab a few to play with

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type these in:

```
/give slab
/give stairs
/give door
```

(You can craft them too — three stone in a row makes slabs, stone in a staircase
shape makes stairs, and a 2-wide, 3-tall block of oak planks makes a door. The
recipe book shows you the shapes. But `/give` is quicker while you're finding
your feet.)

## Lay a slab — half a block

Look at the **top** of a block and place a **slab**. See? It only fills the
**bottom half**. Now look at the **underside** of a block above you and place one
— it clings to the **ceiling** instead. Aim at the **middle of a side** and it
stands up on its edge.

That's the whole trick: a slab fills the half **nearest where you pointed**.
Steps, ledges, half-height walls — all just slabs, placed where you looked.

## Run some stairs

Face the way you want to **walk up**, and place a **stair**. The step appears
facing you. Stack a few — up and along, up and along — and you've got a
staircase you can stroll straight up. No jumping needed.

## Hang a door

Stand in your doorway and place a **door** — mind your head, it's **two blocks
tall**. Now **right-click** it. Open. Right-click again — shut. Walk through while
it's open; try while it's shut and you'll bump your nose. That's a real door,
doing a real door's job.

Pop a **second door** right beside the first and they swing open together — a
proper double doorway.

*You did it.* That's the backbone of building sorted. When you fancy more, there
are **fence gates** for your garden, **trapdoors** for hatches, **glass panes**
that join into windows, and **signs** you can write on — they all work by the
same two ideas you just learned: *place it where you want it*, and *right-click
the ones that move*.
