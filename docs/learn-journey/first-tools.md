# Make Your First Tools

*Bare hands are slow going. Let's make some proper tools — then upgrade them to stone, which are faster and tougher.*

You'll want your **Workbench** down and a few **sticks** ready. Stand next to
it and **right-click** it to open the 3×3 grid.

## Wooden tools first

Every tool is a **head** (planks, for wooden ones) plus **sticks.** Here are the
shapes — `P` is a plank, `S` is a stick, `[ ]` is empty:

```
Pickaxe        Axe          Shovel       Sword
[P][P][P]      [P][P]       [P]          [P]
[ ][S][ ]      [P][S]       [S]          [P]
[ ][S][ ]      [ ][S]       [S]          [S]
```

Make at least a **pickaxe** (for stone), an **axe** (for wood), and a **sword**
(for tonight — trust me). A **shovel** is lovely for dirt and sand too.

## Mine some stone

Stone is just under the grass almost everywhere, or in any hillside or cave. With
your **wooden pickaxe** selected, **hold the left mouse button** on stone until it
breaks — it drops **cobblestone.** Grab a good stack, eight or ten.

## Now, stone tools

Same shapes as before, but swap the **planks in the head for cobblestone:**

```
Stone Pickaxe
[C][C][C]
[ ][S][ ]
[ ][S][ ]
C = Cobblestone   S = Stick
```

Stone tools mine **faster** and last **far longer** (a wooden pickaxe gives up
after 59 uses; stone keeps going for 131). And you'll **need at least a stone
pickaxe** to get anything out of **iron ore** later.

## 🎉 You did it

A full set of stone tools — you can now gather almost everything up here on the
surface, and you're ready to start digging for iron.
