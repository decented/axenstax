# Cook on a Campfire

*Raw meat barely fills you. Cooked food fills you up properly — and a campfire is how you cook. Let's get one going.*

(Quick note: the campfire is for *food.* The furnace is a different thing — that's
for melting ore.)

## Gather your bits

You'll need **sticks** and **logs** from any tree, and some **raw meat** — give a
cow, pig, sheep or chicken a few punches and it'll drop some.

## Craft the campfire

At your **Crafting Table** (the 3×3 grid):

```
[S][S][S]
[L][L][L]
[S][S][S]
S = Stick    L = Log
```

That makes one **Campfire** (unlit for now). **Right-click** to place it on the ground.

## Keep it fed

Drop in some **fuel** first — a log burns for about 30 seconds, coal for around 4
minutes. A campfire won't light without fuel already loaded, so this part comes
before you strike a flame.

## Light it

With fuel in, it's still cold until you light it. Two ways to get a flame:

- **Flint & Steel** — the easy way. Right-click the campfire with it and it lights
  straight away. (You make Flint & Steel from **flint** — which comes from breaking
  **gravel**, though it's only about a **15% drop** so you may need to break a few —
  plus an **iron ingot.**)
- **Rub a stick** — the no-iron way. Hold a **Stick** and **right-click-and-hold**
  on the campfire for about **five seconds.** It catches roughly seven tries out of
  ten (the stick gets used up either way), so don't worry if it takes a couple of goes.

It only cooks while it's lit and fuelled.

## Cook and eat

1. Hold a piece of **raw meat** and **right-click** the lit fire to lay it on —
   there's room for **four** at once.
2. Give it about **ten seconds** each.
3. **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the cooked food off.
4. Select it in your hotbar and **right-click** (while you're hungry) to eat.

Watch that hunger bar climb. 🍖

## 🎉 You did it

You can keep yourself fed now — which matters, because hunger never stops creeping down.
