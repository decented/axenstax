# Make It Your Own

*Don't like how a block looks? Change it. In the Workshop you can blow a block up huge, paint it however you fancy, and then wear that new look on every block of that kind in your world. Let's give it a go.*

The **Workshop** is your own private design room — a calm, empty space just for making things look the way *you* want. Nothing you do here can break your real world, so play around as much as you like.

## Step into the Workshop

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type:

```
/ws
```

You'll pop into a blank room with a floor and nothing else. You're in Creative here, so you can fly and you've got blocks to spare. Look in your **last hotbar slot** — that funny little tool is the **Bellows.** That's what does the magic.

## Place a block and blow it up

1. Pick a block from your hotbar — **stone** is perfect for a first try — and **right-click** to place it on the floor.
2. Select the **Bellows.**
3. Aim at the block you just placed. A **wireframe cage** appears, showing where it's about to grow — **green** means there's room, **red** means something's in the way (just give it more space).
4. **Hold down right-click.** Watch it swell — bigger… bigger… *huge*, four blocks tall. Keep holding right through all three puffs until it locks at full size.

Let go too soon and it shrinks straight back down — no harm done, just aim and hold again. Once it's locked, you can let go and walk all the way around it.

*(Stuck on bare floor? Place a block first, then aim the Bellows **at the block**, not the ground.)*

## Paint it with a dye

Now the fun bit. You paint with **dyes** — grab whichever colour you like (press **B** to open your item explorer and pull out a dye, say **Red Dye**).

Hold the dye and **left-click** a square on the giant block. That little square turns your colour. Daub away — square by square, paint a face, paint a pattern, paint a smiley.

## Press V to sculpt

You're in **Paint** mode by default, which is great for colouring but won't reshape anything — left- and right-clicking there only paints. To add or carve off chunks of the block, **press V** to switch to **Sculpt** mode first.

Once you're in Sculpt mode:

- Want to *add* a chunky bit? **Right-click** with the dye and a new coloured cube pops out where you're aiming.
- Made a wonky cube? Switch to your **empty hand** (or a pickaxe) and **left-click** it to carve it back off.

Press **V** again any time to flip back to Paint mode and keep colouring.

## Two clever tricks

These two make life so much easier:

- **Eyedropper — press G.** Aim at a square that's already the perfect colour and press **G** to *grab* that colour. Now you're painting with it — no hunting for the right dye.
- **Mirror — press M.** Press **M** to flip on symmetry. Now whatever you paint on one side copies to the other side automatically — so you only do half the work and it still comes out matching. Press **M** again to cycle through the mirror modes, or turn it off.

## Pin it on — press P

Happy with your masterpiece? **Press P.**

That's the magic moment: **every single block of that kind in your world now wears your new look.** Pin a painted stone, and all your stone changes at once. It only changes how blocks *look* — they still behave exactly the same — so it's completely safe.

The giant block deflates back to normal size, and your design is saved.

## Keep your looks in the Wardrobe

You don't only get *one* design — you can make loads and switch between them whenever you fancy.

Press **K** to open your **Wardrobe.** It's a little gallery of every look you've pinned, sorted by block. In here you can:

- **Set active** — choose which saved look is being worn right now.
- **Use original** — go back to the plain, stock block any time. Nothing's ever lost.
- **Rename** or **delete** your designs to keep things tidy.

Press **K** again to close it.

## 🎉 You did it

You blew a block up, painted it, and made your whole world wear the look you dreamed up — and you can swap it out from the Wardrobe whenever the mood takes you. That's *your* world now, looking exactly how you want it.
