# How to Win Satori Rush

*Start with nothing. Dig all the way down. Pull up the rarest block in the world — the Satori — as fast as you can.*

## Starting a game

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type:

```
/scenario satori-rush
```

You start with an **empty bag** in a fresh survival world. There's **no time
limit** — the clock counts **up**, and a lower time is a better run. It only ticks
**while you're playing**, and the world **saves**, so you can stop and pick it up
later. It's a proper, full survival climb — nothing's made easier. The instant you
get your first Satori, the **Genesis Block** fanfare plays and your time goes on the
board (carry on playing if you like).

## The climb, step by step

Satori sits at the very bottom of the world, so this is the whole tool-tier journey.
Survive and craft your way down:

1. **Punch a tree** → logs → **planks** → **sticks** → a **Crafting Table.** (Same
   as [Make Your First Workbench](first-workbench.md).)
2. **Wooden pickaxe** → mine **stone** → **stone tools.** (Same as [Make Your First
   Tools](first-tools.md).)
3. **Dig down carefully** — make a staircase, never straight down (lava and long
   drops live below). Take **torches** so you can see.
4. **Find iron ore** and mine it with your **stone pickaxe** (a wooden one won't do
   it). Build a **Furnace** and **smelt** the raw iron into **iron ingots.**
5. **Make an iron pickaxe**, then go **deeper** for **diamond ore** (down below about
   Y15 — check your Y on the **F3** overlay). Mine diamonds with the iron pickaxe.
6. **Make a diamond pickaxe** — you'll need it for the last step.

## Mining the Satori — the rules that trip people up

Satori is rare and fussy. **All** of these have to be true at once:

- You're **deep — Y 9 or below** (watch the **Y** number on **F3**).
- You're holding a **diamond pickaxe** (or better). An iron one will **not** drop a Satori.
- It only comes from **pure deepslate** — the plain dark rock down there, **not** any ore.
- It hides in rare **veins** (about 1 in 100,000), so you'll mine a *lot* of deepslate.
- **Freshly-dug only.** Deepslate already exposed in a cave wall will **never** give a
  Satori — you have to **break into new rock yourself** and keep digging.

So: get down to Y≤9 with a diamond pickaxe and **mine fresh pure deepslate,
steadily.** Keep at it — a vein will turn up, and your first Satori is yours.

## Staying alive all the way down

- **Eat.** Cook food early ([campfire](cook-on-a-campfire.md)) and carry it down with you.
- **Light everything** with torches — to see, and to keep night-time things off you.
- **Mind the lava** deep down. Carry spare blocks to wall it off, and never tunnel
  blindly straight down.

## If you're stuck

- **Iron pickaxe on deepslate** and no Satori — you need **diamond.**
- **Mining cave-wall deepslate** — it's "stale" and won't drop. Dig **fresh.**
- **Not deep enough** — if F3 shows Y above 9, keep going down.
- **Falling into lava** with all your kit — slow and steady wins this one.

## 🎉 You did it

First Satori in the world = the **Genesis Block**, and your time's on the board. This
is the hardest thing in the whole game — seriously well done. 🟧
