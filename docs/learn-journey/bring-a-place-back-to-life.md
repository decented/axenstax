# Bring a Place Back to Life

*You can mine, build, cook, fight and farm. Now for the best bit: making somewhere feel alive again. Birds, flowers, trees, water, animals and friends. Let's bring a grey patch of ground back to life.*

## Find a grey patch

Every part of the world has a life of its own. Plants, trees, water and animals make it thrive. Bare, stripped ground makes it go **grey**. The colour drains out, the plants stand still and the birds go quiet.

- **Look for one.** If you've been digging, a patch of ground you've stripped bare may already have lost its colour. Grey places are easy to spot.
- **Or make one.** Pick a patch about the size of a big garden. Dig up the grass and plants and leave bare stone and gravel. The colour fades slowly, so give it a good while.
- **Play in Survival.** In Creative the world stays colourful, so nothing goes grey there.

Want a ready-made grey place? Press **J** to open the Trials. Once a place in your world has gone grey, or you've finished the **Power Plant** Trial, the **Grey Quarry** is waiting on the **Bloom** shelf. It's a stripped quarry with everything you need in your bag.

## Plant more than one kind of tree

Different kinds of life count more than lots of the same kind. So don't plant a whole forest of one tree.

1. Plant **oak**, **birch** and **spruce** saplings, or whichever kinds you can find. Saplings need **Dirt** or **Grass** to grow on, so lay some dirt down on bare stone.
2. Give them room.

Trees are the slow part, so plant them first.

## Plant a crop

Use a **Hoe** on some dirt and plant **seeds**, just like in **[Start Your First Farm](your-first-farm.md)**. Try two kinds, wheat and carrots maybe. A mixed field is a healthy field.

## Add water

Water is life. Pour water from a **bucket** to make a little pond, or let a stream run through. Water counts, and animals love it.

## Bring a couple of animals

Walk a few animals into your patch. Two **different** kinds, such as a sheep and a cow, beat two of the same. A **Lead** lets you walk them over. Villagers are people, not wildlife, so they don't count here.

## Listen

Now wait, and keep your ears open. In the **daytime**, when it isn't raining, you may hear **birdsong**. You'll see the colour come back, the grass sway, and pollen drift past. At night, listen for **crickets** and look for **fireflies**.

A place comes back faster than it fades. Be patient, because trees take their time.

## Hold a Feast

Last, share something. A **Feast** is a meal for everyone nearby, and it lifts the place for a while.

1. Craft a **Feast Table**: three planks on top, and below them a stick, a loaf of **bread**, and a stick.
2. Place it. Hold a stack of at least **four** things to eat.
3. Have a **friend** nearby, or a **villager**. **Right-click** the table.

The food is shared out, confetti pops, and the table becomes a **Feast Marker** with a name like "The Feast of Long Hollow". If nobody's close enough, the game says: "A feast needs guests. Bring friends, or find a village." A place can hold one Feast a day.

## Blocks and ideas

*A little card to keep.*

A **block** you give away is gone from your hands. If you hand over your stone, you don't have it any more.

An **idea** is different. A **Plan** of something you built doesn't get used up when you build from it. It can be shared, and it keeps its **credit line**, so whoever had the idea is never forgotten. Blocks run out and ideas don't.

## 🎉 You did it, that's the thirteen!

You've brought a place back to life. That's what this game is about: making things, and looking after the places you make them in.

Now go and play. **[Make It Your Own](make-it-your-own.md)**, **[Save Your First Build](capture-a-schematic.md)**, or just wander.
