# How to Win Hash Dash

*Three minutes. Mine as much hard rock as you can. Here's exactly how to top the board.*

## Starting a game

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type:

```
/scenario hash-dash
```

You start **completely empty-handed** — no tools, no food, nothing in your
hotbar. A clock counts down from **3:00.** When it hits zero, whoever's racked
up the most **work** wins. Don't worry about your save — Hash Dash runs on
your world but doesn't touch it.

## How "work" is scored — read this bit

Every block you break adds **work**, and **harder blocks are worth loads more** —
but stone and ore need the right pickaxe tier to actually *harvest*; bare fists
just bounce off them for **nothing**. Logs, dirt, sand, and the like you can
punch straight away. Here's the pecking order, best first:

| Block | Work each |
|---|---|
| **Pure deepslate** | ~178 (the best!) |
| **Stone** | ~109 |
| Logs / deepslate ore | ~43 |
| Coal / iron / diamond ore | ~26 |
| Dirt / grass | ~22 |
| Cobblestone / planks | ~16 |
| Sand / gravel | ~4 |
| Leaves / snow | ~1 |

The takeaway: **don't waste a single second on soft blocks.** Spend the whole three
minutes on the hardest rock you can break.

## The winning plan

Because you start with **nothing**, the real game is a sprint up the tool tree
before the clock runs out — every second spent geared up with a stone pickaxe
is a second you're *not* punching trees.

1. **Punch a tree, fast.** Bare-handed logs are slow but they're your only way
   in — grab **4 or 5** and move on. (Bare fists can't harvest stone or ore at
   all, so don't waste swings on rock yet.)
2. **Craft planks and sticks**, then a **Crafting Table**, and place it down.
   Without a table you're stuck with a cramped 2×2 craft — no pickaxe fits in that.
3. **Craft a Wood Pickaxe** at the table. Now — and only now — you can actually
   harvest stone.
4. **Mine stone until you can craft a Stone Pickaxe**, then swap up straight
   away. It breaks stone quicker, which matters for every second left on the clock.
5. **Now mine stone, stone, stone.** It's right under the grass, in every
   hillside, all through caves — and at ~109 each it's your bread and butter.
6. **Dig into a hillside or cave**, not a pit — you want a wall of rock in front of
   you so you never stop swinging.
7. **See deepslate?** (Deep caves and ravines.) Go for it — at ~178 it's the best
   block going. But only if it's close; digging all the way down isn't worth it in
   three minutes.
8. **Never stop moving toward the next swing.** Walking, jumping, rummaging in
   your bag — all wasted seconds once you're geared up. Crosshair on hard rock,
   left button held.

## If you're not winning

- **Dawdling on the wood stage** — every second before you've got a stone
  pickaxe is a second you can't score on stone or ore. Punch, craft, place table,
  craft pickaxe, go.
- **Mining dirt, sand, or leaves** once you're geared up — almost zero points.
  Head for stone.
- **Wandering about** hunting for "good" blocks — plain stone is already great, and
  it's under your feet.
- **Digging straight down** and falling. No need to go deep; stay on rock you can reach.

## 🎉 You did it

Most work when the buzzer goes wins. Mine the hard stuff, skip the soft stuff, keep
swinging.
