# Film Your World

*Built something you're proud of? Let's film it. Axe'n'Stax has a built-in **Director** — a camera you can fly anywhere, with no walls in its way — for capturing beautiful flythroughs and slow circles around your builds. It's the same idea as the freecam in your favourite Minecraft videos, only built right in.*

> **Heads up:** the Director isn't part of the web taster — it lives in the free desktop app. It's brand new, so the keys below might still change a little.

## Lift off — press F6

Press **F6.** Your character freezes on the spot and the camera floats free — now *you* are the camera. (Don't worry, your character is safe and waiting; press **F6** again any time to drop straight back in.)

Have a fly around to find your feet:

- **W A S D** — move where you're looking
- **Mouse** — look around
- **Space** — go up, **Left Shift** — go down
- Hold **Left Ctrl** — zoom along faster

The camera glides straight through walls, so you can get anywhere.

## Tidy the screen — press F8

Press **F8** and the hotbar, hearts and crosshair all melt away — just your world, ready for its close-up. Press **F8** again whenever you want them back.

## Circle your build — the hero shot

The camera has a few **modes**, and **F9** flips between them. There's no label on screen yet (that's coming), so you tell them apart by what the camera *does*.

Keep pressing **F9** until the camera **locks onto your character** — now moving the mouse **swings the camera in a circle around you.** That's **Look-at** mode (it's the fourth press after you open the Director).

- **Move the mouse** to swing around.
- Press **W** to drift in closer, **S** to pull back.

A slow circle around your build, with you standing in the middle of it, is the shot that makes a world look amazing.

## Make a gliding flythrough

Want the camera to travel a smooth path all on its own? You drop a few "stops" and it joins them up:

1. Press **F9** until you can **steer freely again** (that's **Free-fly**).
2. Fly to where you want the shot to **begin**, and press **Enter** — that pins a stop right there.
3. Fly on a little and press **Enter** again. Drop three or four stops along the route you'd like the camera to travel.
4. Press **F12.** The camera takes over and **glides smoothly through every stop in order.** Press **F12** again to stop it.

Don't love it? Press **Backspace** to wipe the path and try a different route. Hide the screen with **F8** while it plays, and you've got a proper cinematic flythrough.

## Keep the footage

The easiest way to *save* a clip right now is to **screen-record** it: start **OBS** (or any screen recorder), hide the screen with **F8**, and play your shot.

You can also tap **F4** to save a recording of your whole session — a little message tells you where it went. That's for an exciting feature **coming soon**: replaying your session and re-filming it in slow motion. For now, screen-recording is the way to get a video.

## Back to the action — press F6

Press **F6** to hop back into your character. The screen comes back and you carry on right where you left off.

## 🎉 You did it

You flew the camera, cleared the screen, circled your build like a pro, and set a gliding flythrough running. That's everything you need to start making videos of your world.
