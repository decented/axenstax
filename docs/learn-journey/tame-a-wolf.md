# Tame a Wolf

*Everything so far, you've done alone. Time to change that. A wolf you've tamed trots along with you, sits when you ask, and piles into any fight on your side. Your first real friend in the world.*

## Find a wolf

Wolves are grey, about knee-height, and wander on their own like the other animals. Look in:

- **Taiga** and **Snowy Tundra** — your best bet, there are plenty
- **Forest** — you'll still spot a few

They're as tough as you are, and they won't bother you — they just pad around.

## Get some bones

You tame a wolf by feeding it **Bones.** Bones drop from:

- **Bears** and **Hyenas** — the night-time nasties, and your richest source
- **Cows, pigs and sheep** — every so often (about one kill in eight)

Bring a **few**, because taming isn't a sure thing on the first go.

## Win it over

Hold a **Bone** and **right-click the wolf.** Each try has about a **one-in-three** chance of working — and the bone is used up whether it works or not, so don't be put off if it takes a couple. Keep at it.

When it works you'll see **"Tamed! The wolf is yours."** — and that's that, it's bonded to you for keeps. The game even remembers it's yours after you save and come back.

## What your wolf does

The moment it's tamed, your wolf **sticks close** — trotting a few blocks behind and catching up if it falls back. From then on:

- **It fights what you fight.** Swing at a Bear and your wolf wades in too.
- **It defends you.** If something hits you, your wolf goes straight after whatever did it.
- **Tell it to wait** — **right-click your own wolf** to make it **Sit.** A sitting wolf stays put no matter how far you wander. Right-click again to call it back up.

## Look after it

A tamed wolf is **loss, not loot** — if it's killed it drops **nothing**, and you've lost a friend. So don't lead it into a fight it can't win, and let it **sit** somewhere safe when you're off doing something dangerous.

## 🎉 You did it

You've got a companion. The world feels a lot less lonely — and a fair bit less dangerous — with a wolf at your heel.
