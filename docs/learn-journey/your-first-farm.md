# Start Your First Farm

*A farm means you never go hungry and never run short on supplies. Let's grow your first crop, start to finish.*

We'll grow **Hemp** first, because you can find its seeds in the wild straight away.
The exact same steps work for **Cotton**, the **dye flowers**, and food crops once
you've got their seeds.

## Find some seeds

Wander the grass and look for little wild plants:

- **Hemp** — grows wild in **Plains** and **Forest.** Break it for **Hemp Seeds.**
- **Cotton** — grows wild in the **Savanna.** Break it for **Cotton Seeds.**
- **Flowers** (cornflower, field poppy, buttercup) — dotted across the grass. Break
  them for flower seeds.

Break a couple of hemp plants to get a little handful of seeds.

> **Heads up:** food crops (wheat, carrot, potato, corn) grow exactly the same way,
> but their seeds don't turn up in the wild just yet — so start with hemp, cotton, or
> flowers, which you *can* find.

## Make a hoe and till the soil

Craft a **Hoe** at your table (two head pieces on top, two sticks below). Then
**right-click the top of a dirt or grass block** with it — it turns into **Tilled
Soil**, dark and ready to plant.

Till a little row of four to six blocks. Here's a tip: tilling right next to
**water** makes crops grow **twice as fast**, so dig a small water channel beside
your row if you can manage it.

## Plant your seeds

Hold your **Hemp Seeds** and **right-click the tilled soil.** A tiny sprout pops up.
Plant the whole row.

## Wait for them to grow

Crops grow through **four stages** and want **light** (open sky is plenty — they'll
even grow overnight under the stars). Each stage is about **ten seconds**, or
**five** if there's water within a few blocks. Go and do something else, then come back.

In a hurry? **Fertiliser** (made from **Magnesium** + **Sulphur**) bumps a crop up a
stage each time you right-click it.

## Harvest, then plant again

When a plant reaches its **full-grown** look, **break it** (left-click). It drops the
crop — **Hemp Fibre** — plus **a seed or two**, so you can plant straight back. The
soil resets, ready for the next one.

Hemp Fibre twists into **Rope** (3 fibre) and **Canvas** (4 fibre). Cotton makes
**String** and **Cloth.** Flowers make **dyes.**

## 🎉 You did it

You've grown and harvested your first crop, with seeds in hand to keep it going. A
farm beside water never runs dry.
