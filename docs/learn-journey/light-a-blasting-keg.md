# Light a Blasting Keg

*Fancy blowing something up? Properly this time — not painting over a block, but a
real keg, a hissing fuse, and a crater. We'll make one, light it by hand, and then
do the clever version where you set it off from a safe distance with a plunger.*

Here's the thing worth knowing first: a blast **breaks** blocks but you get
**nothing back** from them. It's **demolition, not mining** — the pickaxe is still
the only way to actually *collect* anything. So blowing a tunnel is fast, but you
wave goodbye to whatever was in the wall. Fair trade for the fun.

## Grab the parts

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type this in:

```
/give blasting_keg
```

(You can make the keg properly too — mine a bit of **Brimstone** and **Nitre**,
turn them with some coal into **Black Powder**, and wrap that in planks. The
recipe book walks you through it. But `/give` is quicker while you're learning.)

You'll need a **Magnesium Firestarter** too, and that one you have to craft — put
a **Magnesium** and an **Iron Ingot** side by side in the top row of your
crafting grid.

## Set your keg down

Pop a **Blasting Keg** on some open ground, well away from your house or anything
you'd miss. Right now it's just a quiet wooden barrel. It won't do a thing until
you light it.

## Light the fuse — then run

**Right-click the keg with the Magnesium Firestarter.** You'll hear the fuse catch.
You've got about **four seconds** — so don't admire your work, **leg it**. Get a
few blocks back, behind a wall if there's one handy.

*Boom.* A crater roughly eight blocks across (the blast reaches about four blocks
in every direction from the keg), right where it was. And notice — no
blocks dropped for you to pick up. That's the rule doing its job.

(Aim your crosshair at any lit keg and a little label counts the fuse down for you — handy if you want to know exactly how long you've got.)

*You did it.* Try setting one off next to **bedrock** sometime — the bedrock just
shrugs it off. Some blocks are simply too tough to break.

## The clever version — blow it from a distance

Once that's fun, here's the proper demolition trick:

```
/give blasting_keg
/give plunger
/give cable
```

Place a **keg**, run a line of **Cable** from it to where you'll stand, and put a
**Plunger Detonator** at your end of the wire. Now **push the plunger** — the pulse
races down the cable and lights the keg from right where you're safely standing.

(You can craft the Plunger Detonator too — an **Iron Ingot**, a **Copper Cable**,
and an **Oak Plank** in a row across the top of your crafting grid.)

Run cables to **three or four kegs** from one plunger and a single push sets them
**all** off at once. Or hide a **pressure plate** on the wire and let someone *walk*
into the bang. That's a story you can take as far as you like.
