# Surviving Your First Night

*When the sun goes down, the world gets dark — and things come out in the dark. Let's make sure you're ready before dusk.*

Once it's night, unfriendly things appear: **Brigands**, **Marauders**, **Bears**,
and packs of **Hyenas.** The trick is simple — be ready *before* it's dark.

## Watch the light

The sky dims as evening comes on. That fading light is your timer. Use whatever
daylight is left to get sorted.

## Make some torches

Light is safety, and torches make light. You'll need **coal** (mine **coal ore** —
even a wooden pickaxe does it) and **sticks:**

```
[Coal]
[Stick]
→ 4 Torches
```

Dot torches around wherever you're going to hunker down.

## Throw together a shelter

Nothing fancy needed. Pick one:

- **Wall yourself in** — stack any blocks (dirt, cobblestone, planks) into a little
  box around you, roof included.
- **Dig in** — tunnel two or three blocks into a hillside and seal the doorway
  behind you.

Pop a torch inside so you're not sat in the dark.

## Even better: sleep through it

A **Bed** skips the whole night in one go. Make one at your table:

```
[Wool][Wool][Wool]
[ P  ][ P  ][ P  ]
Wool = from sheep    P = Planks
```

Place the bed and **right-click it after dark.** You'll jump straight to **morning**,
wake up **fully healed**, and your **spawn point** moves to that bed — so if you're
ever knocked out, you'll wake up right back here. (Right-clicking it in daylight does
nothing, mind.)

## If it comes to a fight

Keep your **sword** selected and **left-click** to swing. The good news: **Brigands
and Marauders only chase you at night**, and Brigands run off when they're badly
hurt. The dark-red **Berserker** is the nasty one — always angry — but it's rare and
lives out at bandit hideouts, so you won't bump into one by accident on night one.

## 🎉 You did it

You made it to sunrise. That's the rhythm now: days for gathering and building,
nights for staying safe (or snoozing straight through).
