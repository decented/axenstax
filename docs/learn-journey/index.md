# Welcome — let's get you started

You're about to step into a world made entirely of blocks, where everything you
see you can take apart, carry off, and build into something new. The ground, the
trees, the hills — all yours.

You don't need to know anything yet. We'll walk you in one step at a time, and
each step is short on purpose — keep it open beside the game, on your phone or
right next to the window, and glance over whenever you'd like a hand.

Ready? Here's the path — **twelve little steps**, in order. Each one builds on the last.

1. **Getting Around** — find your feet: move, look around, and open your bag.
2. **Your First Blocks** — break one, carry it, place it back down.
3. **Make a Crafting Table** — turn wood into your crafting table.
4. **Make Your First Tools** — a wooden axe and pickaxe, then stronger stone ones.
5. **Cook on a Campfire** — build a fire, cook some food, and eat.
6. **Surviving Your First Night** — light, a shelter, and a bed before dark.
7. **Keep Your Stuff in a Chest** — a safe box for everything you're not carrying.
8. **Start a Farm** — plant seeds, grow food, harvest it.
9. **Smelt Iron in a Furnace** — melt ore into metal and make iron tools.
10. **Stand Your Ground** — make a sword and win a fight.
11. **Tame a Wolf** — get yourself a companion who fights on your side.
12. **What the Hash Means** — the real Bitcoin maths under every swing.

Got through all twelve? There's more to play with whenever you fancy it:

- **Save Your First Build** — capture a build onto a blueprint and rebuild it anywhere.
- **Build with Shapes** — slabs, stairs, and a door that opens; turn a plain box into a building.
- **Make It Your Own** — blow a block up huge, paint it however you like, and reskin your whole world.
- **Switch On a Lamp** — wire up your first little circuit, a lamp on a switch.
- **Light a Blasting Keg** — make a keg, light the fuse, and blow a crater (then set one off from a safe distance with a plunger).
- **Hash Dash** — a three-minute mining sprint.
- **Satori Rush** — a race to dig up the rarest block of all.
- **Film Your World** *(the free desktop app)* — fly the built-in camera, circle your build for a hero shot, and make a gliding flythrough.

No rush at all. Go at your own pace. We'll be right here beside you.
