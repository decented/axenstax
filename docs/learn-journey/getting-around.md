# Getting Around

*Before you build anything, let's just get comfy moving about. Five minutes and it'll feel like second nature.*

You're standing in the world. Let's learn the handful of things you'll do all the
time — walk, look, hold things, and open your bag.

## Move and look

Give these a try, one at a time:

- **W A S D** — walk forward, left, back, right.
- **Move the mouse** — look around.
- **Space** — jump.
- **Left Shift** — sneak: slower, but you won't walk off the edge of a block (handy up high).
- **Left Ctrl**, or a quick double-tap of **W** — sprint.

Just wander for a bit until it stops feeling fiddly. No goal here — get a feel for it.

## That little + matters

See the small **+** in the middle of your screen? That's your crosshair, and
whatever block sits under it is what you're pointing at — it gets a thin white
outline. You can reach about **five blocks** away; anything further off is out of
reach for now.

## Your hotbar

Along the bottom are **nine slots.** The lit-up one is whatever you're holding.

- **1–9** — jump straight to a slot.
- **Scroll wheel** — flick to the next or previous one.
- **Q** — drop one of whatever's in your hand.

## Open your bag

- **E** opens (and closes) your inventory — your hotbar plus extra storage, and a
  little **2×2 crafting grid** in the corner.
- **Left-click** a stack to pick it up, then left-click another slot to set it down.
  **Hover** over anything to read its name.
- **Esc** closes any screen. When in doubt, press Esc — it's your "back out of this" button.

## Two handy extras

- **B** opens the **Inventory Explorer** — a search box for every block, tool and
  material in the game. Type a few letters to filter. Brilliant for "what's that
  thing called again?"
- **F3** shows your coordinates (X, Y, Z) and which area you're in. Lost? This helps
  you find your way home.

## 🎉 You did it

You can move, look, hold things, and open your bag — everything you need to start
actually *doing* things.
