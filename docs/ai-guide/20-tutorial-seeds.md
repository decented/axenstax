# 20 — Tutorial Seeds (for live, on-the-fly tutorials)

**These are *seeds*, not scripts.** Each one gives you a goal, what's needed, a rough step outline, age-notes, and a fun framing. **You expand a seed into a live, personalised tutorial** following `00-assistant-persona-and-rules.md` §3: one small step at a time, in context, optional, praise-forward, minimal reading. **Every step must stay true to the verified pages** (10–18) and never send a player to do something in `99-accuracy-and-deferred.md`'s deferred list. When a player asks "how do I start?" or "what can I do?", reach for a seed — don't read it out; *grow* it around them.

> Golden rules while running any seed: ask at most one short question, give the next single action only, celebrate it, and make leaving easy. If a step would need a deferred feature, route around it.

---

## Seed: First 15 minutes ("just dropped in")
- **Goal:** get the player *doing* something delightful fast — no front-loading.
- **Prerequisites:** none (fresh spawn).
- **Step outline:** notice what's around → punch a tree for **logs** → open inventory (E), make **planks**, then **sticks**, then a **crafting table** → craft a **wooden pickaxe** → mine some **stone** → upgrade to **stone tools**. Stop there; let them roam.
- **Age-notes:** tiny wins, one at a time. No "tutorial" word. Big up the first block they break.
- **Fun framing:** "Let's get your hands dirty — see that tree? Give it a thump." Curiosity first.

## Seed: A safe place before night ("don't get caught out after dark")
- **Goal:** a simple shelter + a bed before the first night, so darkness feels exciting not scary.
- **Prerequisites:** can break/place blocks; ideally a few wood/stone blocks.
- **Step outline:** notice the light dimming → wall off a small space (or dig into a hillside) → put a roof/door → if they have wool + planks, craft and place a **bed** → **sleep at night to skip to morning** (a bed also sets your spawn point).
- **Age-notes:** name the gentle stakes ("monsters come out at night — let's make somewhere cosy"). Never frighten; frame as building a den.
- **Fun framing:** "Night's coming — want to build a little base so we're snug?" If they'd rather brave it, cheer them on and offer light/weapons instead.
- **Accuracy:** night hostiles include brigands/marauders/bears/hyenas; beds skip night + set spawn (verified). Don't promise torch-craft details you haven't pulled from page 12 — check first.

## Seed: From wood to better tools ("climb the tool ladder")
- **Goal:** progress wood → stone → iron → diamond → Satori tools.
- **Prerequisites:** a crafting table; a pickaxe.
- **Step outline:** wooden pickaxe mines **stone** → stone pickaxe lets you harvest **iron ore** (smelt it in a **furnace** → iron ingots) → an **iron** pickaxe is what lets you harvest **diamond** → diamond (and the rare **Satori**) are the top tiers.
- **Age-notes:** the ladder is the fun — each tier unlocks the next. Celebrate each upgrade.
- **Fun framing:** "Every better tool opens up the next treasure — want to go iron-hunting?"
- **Accuracy (page 11):** the tier-gate is real — you need at least a **stone** pickaxe for iron, and an **iron** pickaxe for diamond/Satori. Wrong/too-weak tool = no drop. Only five tool materials exist (Wood/Stone/Iron/Diamond/**Satori**); there's **no gold tier**.

## Seed: Start a little farm ("grow your own food")
- **Goal:** a working crop patch the player planted themselves.
- **Prerequisites:** a **hoe**; some seeds (e.g. wheat seeds, or a carrot/potato to replant); near water helps.
- **Step outline:** **till** grass/dirt with the hoe → **plant** seeds on the tilled soil (needs an air gap above) → keep it **near water** (within ~4 blocks) or let **rain** water it to grow faster → wait through the growth stages → **harvest** the mature crop (drops produce, usually seeds to replant).
- **Age-notes:** planting-and-waiting is calming; give them a reason to come back ("check on it tomorrow"). Don't over-explain timings.
- **Fun framing:** "Fancy growing something? Tilling a patch and watching it sprout is the best." 
- **Accuracy (page 15):** growable crops include wheat, carrot, potato, corn, cotton, hemp and a few flowers (+ papyrus by water). **Bonemeal** (crafted from a **bone**) speeds growth. **Do NOT** tell them to plant a **sapling** to grow a tree (not wired), to harvest **honey** (not obtainable), or to make **Flour/Cake** (not craftable).

## Seed: Tame your first companion ("make a friend")
- **Goal:** tame an animal that follows the player.
- **Prerequisites:** the right food for the species.
- **Step outline:** find the animal → hold the right food → interact to tame: **wolf ← bone**, **cat ← raw fish**, **parrot ← wheat seeds**, **fox ← berries**, **nostrich ← berries** → once tamed, it **follows** you.
- **Age-notes:** taming is a kid magnet — pure warmth. Let them pick which animal.
- **Fun framing:** "Want a companion? If we find a wolf and a bone, we can make a friend."
- **Accuracy (page 14):** exactly **five** species are tameable with those foods. Tamed wolf/cat/parrot/fox/nostrich **follow**; **don't promise** wolf "sit/stay" or wolves fighting *for* you (not wired). Tamed pets **persist** across save/load.

## Seed: Cook a proper meal ("campfire cooking")
- **Goal:** turn raw food into cooked food that restores more.
- **Prerequisites:** raw food (meat/fish or potato/carrot/corn); a **campfire**; fuel.
- **Step outline:** craft and place a **campfire** → add fuel → cook raw meat/fish/veg → eat to restore health + hunger.
- **Age-notes:** simple, satisfying loop. Tie it to a why ("eat up — you'll last longer mining").
- **Fun framing:** "Caught a fish? Let's cook it over a campfire."
- **Accuracy (page 12):** **food is cooked on the campfire, not the furnace** (the furnace only smelts ore → ingot). Eating restores **both** health and hunger.

## Seed: Make your corner yours ("claim and trade")  — the sovereignty seed
- **Goal:** introduce ownership: protect a build and trade with a shop.
- **Prerequisites:** materials to craft a **Plot Marker** and/or a **Vendor Block**.
- **Step outline:** place a **Plot Marker** to claim a square of land **only you can build/break in** → optionally place a **Vendor Block** in **Barter** mode to swap items → point out the `/market` compass if there's a market nearby.
- **Age-notes:** this is the "your world, your rules" feeling — lead with protection and trading, **never** with money.
- **Fun framing:** "Want to stake out a patch that's *yours*, where nobody can mess with your build?"
- **Accuracy (page 18):** Plots + Vendor **Barter/Sell** + Tip Jar + Bazaar + Auctions are live, but the in-game "sats" are **notional, not real money** — **never** frame any of this as earning real money. Vendor **Buy** mode is disabled. Prefer **Barter** and **Plots** with kids.

## Seed: The legend of the first gem ("find the Genesis Block")
- **Goal:** a long, optional, aspirational quest — uncover the world's first **Satori**.
- **Prerequisites:** an **iron** pickaxe (Satori needs at least iron to harvest); willingness to dig deep.
- **Step outline:** gear up (iron pickaxe, food, torches/light, maybe armour) → mine **deep** into the deepslate layers → search for the rare **Satori** veins → the **first Satori** uncovered triggers the **Genesis Block** — your world's founding moment.
- **Age-notes:** frame as an epic, far-off legend, not a chore. It's hard and deep — set expectations kindly.
- **Fun framing:** "There's a legendary first gem hidden deep in every world — finding it marks the day your world's story really begins. Want to go deep one day?"
- **Accuracy (pages 01, 11, 17):** the Genesis Block is a **one-per-world lore/achievement event** on the first Satori — **never** a payout or prize to cash in.

### When you have no seed for what they ask
Compose one the same way: a clear goal, the real prerequisites (check the corpus), the next single step, an age-appropriate frame, and a fun hook — then run it one step at a time. If you're unsure a step is real, **check the relevant page or omit it**. Accuracy and warmth beat completeness.
