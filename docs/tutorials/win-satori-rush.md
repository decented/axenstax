# How to Win Satori Rush

> **Goal:** Start from **nothing** and mine your very first **Satori** — claiming
> this world's **Genesis Block** — as fast as you can.

## Start the game

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type:

```
/scenario satori-rush
```

You start with an **empty inventory** in a brand-new survival world. There's
**no time limit** — a clock counts **up**, and a lower time is a better run. The
clock only ticks **while you're playing**, and the world **saves**, so you can
quit and pick the run back up later. It's a real, full survival climb — nothing
is made easier. The moment you get your first Satori, the **Genesis Block**
fanfare plays and your time is recorded (you can keep playing afterwards).

## The climb — step by step

Satori sits at the very bottom of the world, so this is a full tool-tier
journey. Survive and craft your way down:

1. **Punch a tree** → logs. Make **planks**, then **sticks**, then a **Crafting
   Table**. (See **[Make Your First Workbench](first-workbench.md)**.)
2. **Wooden pickaxe** → mine **stone** → make **stone tools**. (See
   **[Make Your First Tools](first-tools.md)**.)
3. **Dig down carefully** — staircase down, never straight down (lava and long
   drops live below). Use **torches** so you can see.
4. **Find iron ore** and mine it with your **stone pickaxe** (a wooden one won't
   work). Build a **Furnace** and **smelt** the raw iron into **iron ingots**.
   (See **[Smelting](../../player-guide/player-guide/smelting.md)**.)
5. **Make an iron pickaxe.** Now go **deeper** to find **diamond ore** (it's down
   near the bottom, below about Y15 — check Y with **F3**). Mine diamonds with
   the iron pickaxe.
6. **Make a diamond pickaxe** — you need it for the final step.

## Mining the Satori — the rules that catch people out

Satori is rare and fussy. **All** of these must be true:

- You must be **deep — at Y 9 or below** (watch the **Y** number on the **F3**
  overlay).
- You must be holding a **diamond pickaxe** (or better). An iron pickaxe will
  **not** drop a Satori.
- Satori only comes from **pure deepslate** — the plain dark rock down there,
  **not** from any ore.
- It only appears in hidden **veins** (very rare, about 1 in 100,000), so you
  have to mine a *lot* of deepslate.
- **Freshly-dug only:** deepslate you find already exposed in a cave wall will
  **never** drop a Satori. You have to **dig fresh deepslate yourself** — break
  into new rock and keep digging.

So: get to Y≤9 with a diamond pickaxe and **mine fresh pure deepslate steadily**.
Keep going — a vein will turn up, and the first Satori is yours.

## Surviving the whole way

- **Eat.** Cook food early (**[campfire tutorial](cook-on-a-campfire.md)**) and
  carry it down.
- **Light everything** with torches — both to see and to keep night-time mobs
  off you.
- **Beware lava** deep down. Carry spare blocks to wall it off, and never tunnel
  straight down into the unknown.

## Common mistakes / if you're stuck

- **Using an iron pickaxe on deepslate** and wondering why no Satori — you need
  **diamond**.
- **Mining deepslate you found in a cave** — it's "stale" and won't drop. Dig
  **fresh** rock.
- **Not deep enough.** If F3 shows Y above 9, keep going down.
- **Falling into lava** with all your gear. Slow and steady wins this one.

## You did it!

First Satori in the world = the **Genesis Block**, and your time is on the board.
This is the hardest thing in the game — well done. 🟧
