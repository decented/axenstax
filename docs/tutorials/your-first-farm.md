# Start Your First Farm

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have tilled some soil, planted seeds,
> grown a crop, and harvested it — the loop that feeds and supplies you forever.

We'll grow **Hemp** first, because you can find its seeds in the wild right away.
The exact same steps work for **Cotton**, the **dye flowers**, and food crops
once you have their seeds.

## 1. Find seeds in the wild

Walk around the grass and look for small wild plants:

- **Hemp plant** — grows wild in **Plains** and **Forest**. Break it for **Hemp
  Seeds**.
- **Cotton plant** — grows wild in the **Savanna**. Break it for **Cotton
  Seeds**.
- **Flowers** (cornflower, field poppy, buttercup) — dotted on grass. Break them
  for flower seeds.

Break a couple of hemp plants to get a handful of seeds.

> **Heads up:** food crops (wheat, carrot, potato, corn) grow exactly the same
> way, but right now their seeds don't appear in the wild — so start with hemp,
> cotton, or flowers, which you *can* find.

## 2. Make a hoe and till the soil

Craft a **Hoe** at your table (two head pieces on top, two sticks below — see
**[Crafting](../../player-guide/player-guide/crafting.md)**). Then **right-click
the top of a dirt or grass block** with the hoe. It turns into **Tilled Soil** —
dark, ready-to-plant earth.

Till a little row of 4–6 blocks. Tilling soil next to **water** makes crops grow
**twice as fast**, so dig a small water channel beside your row if you can.

## 3. Plant the seeds

Hold your **Hemp Seeds** and **right-click the tilled soil**. A tiny sprout
appears in the block above. Plant your whole row.

## 4. Wait for them to grow

Crops grow through **4 stages** and need **light** (open sky is plenty — they
even grow overnight under the sky). Each stage takes about **10 seconds**, or
**5 seconds** if there's water within a few blocks. Go do something else and
come back.

You can rush a crop along by right-clicking it with **Fertiliser** (made from
**Magnesium** + **Sulphur**) — each one bumps it up a stage.

## 5. Harvest and replant

When a plant reaches its **full-grown** look, **break it** (left-click). It
drops the crop — **Hemp Fibre** — plus **a seed or two** so you can plant again.
The soil resets to tilled, ready for the next seed.

Hemp Fibre twists into **Rope** (3 fibre) and **Canvas** (4 fibre). Cotton makes
**String** and **Cloth**. Flowers make **dyes**.

## You did it! 🎉

You've grown and harvested your first crop, and you've got seeds to keep going.
A farm next to water never runs dry.
