# Capture Your First Schematic

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have saved a small build onto a blueprint,
> developed it in the sun, and rebuilt it somewhere else — exactly the same size.

A **schematic** is a saved copy of a build. You capture it once, then you can
rebuild it whenever you like. Let's do a tiny one first.

## 1. Build something tiny

Make a little structure on flat ground — a 3×3 box with walls, or a tiny tower.
Keep it small for your first go.

## 2. Get Blueprint Paper

Craft **Blueprint Paper** (a **Papyrus Sheet**, an **Iron Ingot**, and **Salt**
in a column — see **[Crafting](../../player-guide/player-guide/crafting.md)**).
One craft gives you a few sheets. You'll also need a **Drafting Stamp** — the
reusable tool that actually does the capturing. Craft one (see
**[Crafting](../../player-guide/player-guide/crafting.md)**), or in Creative grab
it from the Inventory Explorer (**B**).

## 3. Lay the paper and capture

1. **Place Blueprint Paper flat on the ground** all around the base of your
   build (the ground under each sheet must be solid, with open air above it).
2. Hold your **Drafting Stamp** and **right-click your build** (any block of it
   — the Stamp looks straight down to find the paper underneath). The Stamp is a
   reusable tool and is **not** used up. *(An empty hand does nothing now — the
   Drafting Stamp is what captures.)*
3. A capture dialog opens — give your build a **name**, pick a **licence**, and
   confirm.

The paper is used up, and a **plan** appears in your bag. Capture is **1:1** —
it records your build exactly. It even works underground; you don't need
sunlight to capture.

## 4. Develop it in the sunshine

Your fresh plan is **undeveloped**. To finish it:

1. Hold the plan and **right-click the ground** under **open sky** to lay down a
   **Latent Print**.
2. Leave it in **daylight**. It needs about **90 seconds of direct sun** — a roof,
   trees, or night will pause it (the progress is saved, so it just waits).
3. When the plan's icon turns full **blue**, it's done. **Right-click the Latent
   Print** to pick the plan back up.

## 5. Rebuild it

1. Hold the **developed** plan and **right-click** → choose **Place in world**.
2. A see-through preview appears. Aim it where you want; press **Q / E** to
   rotate. Green means OK, red means blocked.
3. **Left-click** to start — the build constructs itself, block by block.

In **Survival** you need all the blocks it's made of (they get used up as it
builds). In **Creative** it's free. Either way it rebuilds at the **same size**
you captured. When it finishes, an **Architect Plaque** appears in the middle,
showing who designed it.

## You did it! 🎉

You captured a build, developed it, and rebuilt it. Now you can copy your best
creations anywhere — and if you quit mid-build, it picks up right where it left
off when you come back.
