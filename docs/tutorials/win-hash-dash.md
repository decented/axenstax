# How to Win Hash Dash

> **Goal:** Score the **highest total "work"** before the **3-minute** timer runs
> out. This page is your strategy for doing exactly that.

## Start the game

Open the chat with **T** (or **/**) and type:

```
/scenario hash-dash
```

You start with a **stone pickaxe**, a **stone axe**, and **8 bread**. A clock
counts down from **3:00** (3600 ticks). When it hits zero, whoever has the most
**work** wins. Hash Dash is **transient** — it runs on your world but doesn't
overwrite your save.

## How "work" is scored — this is everything

Every block you break adds **work** to your score, and **harder blocks are worth
far more**. Bare-fist/instant breaks score **nothing** — you must actually
*harvest* blocks. Here's the ranking (highest first):

| Block | Work per block |
|---|---|
| **Pure deepslate** | ~178 (the most!) |
| **Stone** | ~109 |
| Logs / deepslate ore | ~43 |
| Coal/iron/diamond ore | ~26 |
| Dirt / grass | ~22 |
| Cobblestone / planks | ~16 |
| Sand / gravel | ~3 |
| Leaves / snow | ~1 |

The lesson: **don't waste a single second on soft blocks** (leaves, sand, dirt,
gravel). They're almost worthless. Spend the whole 3 minutes on the hardest
blocks you can break.

## The winning plan

1. **Find stone fast.** Stone is everywhere just under the grass, in hillsides,
   and in caves — and at ~109 work each it's your bread-and-butter. With your
   **stone pickaxe** (the best tool for stone), it breaks quickly. Just mine
   stone, stone, stone.
2. **Dig into a hillside or cave**, not a hole — you want a wall of stone in
   front of you so you never stop swinging.
3. **If you can see deepslate** (in a deep cave or ravine), go for it — at ~178
   work it's the single best block in the game. Only chase it if it's close;
   digging all the way down isn't worth it in 3 minutes.
4. **Always be mining.** Walking, jumping, and rummaging in your bag all waste
   seconds. Keep the crosshair on a hard block and keep left-click held.
5. **Eat bread** the moment your hunger dips, so you never slow down. (Tap a
   number key for the bread slot, right-click.)

## Common mistakes / if you're stuck

- **Mining dirt, sand, or leaves.** Almost zero work. Find stone.
- **Wandering around** looking for "good" blocks — plain stone is already great
  and it's right under your feet.
- **Digging straight down** and falling. You don't need to go deep; stay on
  stone you can reach.
- **Ignoring hunger** until you're starving and sluggish — nibble bread early.

## You did it!

Highest work when the buzzer sounds wins. Mine the hard stuff, skip the soft
stuff, keep swinging.
