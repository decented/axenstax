# Film a Cinematic Shot

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have flown the cinematic camera, hidden the
> HUD for a clean frame, orbited your build for a hero shot, and set up a smooth
> dolly flythrough you can play back hands-free.

Axe'n'Stax has a built-in **Director** — a freecam and film studio for capturing
beautiful footage of your world. Let's shoot your first clip.

> **PC desktop only.** The Director is in the native desktop build (not the
> browser version). It's alpha, so these keys may change.

## 1. Build (or find) something worth filming

Stand somewhere with a nice view — a build you're proud of, a cliff, a village.
You don't need anything special; even a few blocks make a good first subject.

## 2. Open the Director

Press **F6**.

Your character **freezes** in place and the camera lifts free of your body. You're
now flying the camera, not walking. (Don't worry — your body is safe and waiting;
**F6** again drops you right back into it.)

Fly around to get a feel for it:

- **W / A / S / D** — move where you're looking
- **Mouse** — look around
- **Space** — up, **Left Shift** — down
- Hold **Left Ctrl** — boost (fly faster)

The camera passes straight through walls, so you can glide anywhere.

## 3. Hide the HUD for a clean frame

Press **F8**.

The hotbar, hearts, and crosshair all vanish — now it's just your world, ready to
film. Press **F8** again any time to bring them back.

## 4. Orbit your build (a hero shot)

**F9** cycles through six camera modes (free-fly → path → tripod → follow →
look-at → POV). There's no on-screen label yet, so you tell them apart by what the
camera does: keep pressing **F9** until the camera **locks onto your character** and
a mouse-move **swings around it** — that's **Look-at** (the fourth press after you
opened the Director).

In **Look-at** the camera keeps its aim on a **target** and orbits it:

1. Press **F10** to pick the target. In single-player that's **you** — your frozen
   avatar, standing in your scene.
2. **Move the mouse** to swing the camera around the target.
3. Press **W** to move in closer, **S** to pull back.

That slow circle around a subject is the classic "hero shot". Try it around your
build with yourself in frame.

## 5. Set up a dolly flythrough

A **dolly** is a smooth camera move you set up once and play back hands-free.

1. Press **F9** until you're back in **Free-fly**.
2. Fly to where you want the shot to **start**, then press **Enter** — that drops a
   *keyframe* (it remembers the camera's exact spot and angle).
3. Fly somewhere else and press **Enter** again. Add a few more along the route you
   want the camera to travel.
4. Press **F12** to **play** it — the camera switches into Path mode on its own and
   glides smoothly through all your keyframes in order. Press **F12** again to stop.
5. Don't like it? Press **Backspace** to clear the path and try again.

With the HUD hidden (**F8**) and a smooth path playing, you've got a polished
flythrough.

## 6. Capture it

The cleanest way to save footage right now is to **screen-record** the Director:
start **OBS** (or any screen recorder), hide the HUD, and play your shot.

You can also press **F4** to save an `.axereplay` recording of the session — that's
for the in-game playback that's **coming soon** (re-fly the Director *through* a
recording, in slow motion). A message tells you where it saved.

## 7. Back to playing

Press **F6** to leave the Director. You drop back into your body, the HUD returns,
and you carry on exactly where you froze.

## You did it! 🎉

You flew the cinematic camera, framed a clean shot, orbited a hero shot, and built
a dolly flythrough. That's everything you need to start making flythroughs and
build tours of your world.
