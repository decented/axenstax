# Cook Food on a Campfire

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have built and lit a **Campfire**, cooked
> raw meat on it, and eaten the cooked food to refill your hunger.

Raw meat fills only a little hunger; **cooked** food fills much more. The
campfire is how you cook. (It's not the furnace — the furnace only smelts ore.)

## 1. Get the ingredients

You'll need **sticks** and **logs** (from any tree). You'll also want some
**raw meat** — punch a cow, pig, sheep, or chicken on the grass and it drops
some.

## 2. Craft the campfire

At your **[Crafting Table](first-workbench.md)** (3×3 grid):

```
[S][S][S]
[L][L][L]
[S][S][S]
S = Stick    L = Log
```

That gives you one (unlit) **Campfire**. Place it on the ground with
**right-click**.

## 3. Light it

The campfire starts **unlit**. Two ways to light it:

- **Flint & Steel** — the easy way. Right-click the campfire with it and it
  lights instantly. (You make Flint & Steel from **flint** + an **iron ingot**;
  flint comes from breaking **gravel**.)
- **Rub a stick** — the no-iron way. Hold a **Stick** and **right-click-and-hold**
  on the campfire for about **5 seconds**. It works about 7 times out of 10
  (the stick gets used up either way).

## 4. Keep it fed

Drop some **fuel** in (a log burns 30 seconds, coal burns 4 minutes). Cooking
only happens while the fire is lit and fuelled.

## 5. Cook and eat

1. Hold a piece of **raw meat** and **right-click** the lit campfire to put it
   on — it has room for **4 things** at once.
2. Wait about **10 seconds** per item.
3. **Right-click with an empty hand** to take the cooked food back.
4. Select it in your hotbar and **right-click** (while hungry) to eat.

Watch your hunger bar climb. 🍖

## You did it! 🎉

You can keep yourself fed now — a huge deal, because hunger never stops ticking.
