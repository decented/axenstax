# Surviving Your First Night

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have made light and a safe spot, and lived
> through your first night — ideally by sleeping in a bed and waking up at dawn.

When the sun goes down it gets **dark**, and dangerous things spawn in the
darkness: **Brigands**, **Marauders**, **Bears**, and packs of **Hyenas**. Get
ready *before* dusk.

## 1. Watch the light

The sky dims as evening comes. That's your timer. Use the daylight you have left
to gather and prepare.

## 2. Make torches

Torches give off light, and light is safety. You'll need **coal** (mine **coal
ore** — even a wooden pickaxe works) and **sticks**:

```
[Coal]
[Stick]
→ 4 Torches
```

Place torches around wherever you're going to hole up.

## 3. Make a shelter

You don't need anything fancy. Pick one:

- **Wall yourself in** — stack any blocks (dirt, cobblestone, planks) into a
  little box around you, with a roof.
- **Dig in** — tunnel two or three blocks into the side of a hill and seal the
  entrance behind you.

Pop a torch inside so you're not sitting in the dark.

## 4. Best option: sleep in a bed

A **Bed** lets you skip the whole night. Make one at your crafting table:

```
[Wool][Wool][Wool]
[ P  ][ P  ][ P  ]
Wool = from sheep    P = Planks
```

Place the bed, and **right-click it after dark**. You'll instantly skip to
**morning**, get **fully healed**, and your **spawn point** is set to that bed —
so if you ever die, you wake up here. (Right-clicking a bed in daytime does
nothing.)

## 5. If you have to fight

Keep your **sword** selected and **left-click** to swing. Good news: **Brigands
and Marauders only chase you at night** and Brigands run away when badly hurt.
The dark-red **Berserker** is the scary one — it's always aggressive — but it's
rare and lives out at bandit hideouts, so you won't meet one by accident on
night one.

## You did it! 🎉

You made it to sunrise. Days are for gathering and building; nights are for
staying safe (or sleeping through them).

food supply.
