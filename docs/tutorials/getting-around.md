# Getting Around

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll be able to walk, look around, pick what
> you're holding from your hotbar, and open your inventory — the four things you
> do constantly in Axe'n'Stax.

## 1. Move and look

- **W / A / S / D** — walk forward, left, back, right.
- **Mouse** — look around (up, down, and turn).
- **Space** — jump.
- **Left Shift** — sneak (slower, and you won't walk off the edge of a block).
- **Left Ctrl** (or double-tap **W**) — sprint.

Walk around for a few seconds until it feels natural.

## 2. The crosshair is what you're pointing at

There's a small **+** in the middle of your screen. Whatever block is under it is
your **target** — it gets a thin white outline. You can only reach about **4
blocks** away; anything further you can't touch yet.

## 3. Your hotbar

Along the bottom of the screen are **9 slots**. The highlighted one is what
you're holding.

- **1–9** — pick a slot.
- **Scroll wheel** — flick to the next/previous slot.
- **Q** — drop one of whatever you're holding.

## 4. Open your inventory

- **E** — open (and close) your inventory. You'll see your hotbar plus more
  storage slots, and a small **2×2 crafting grid** in the corner.
- **Left-click** a stack to pick it up, then left-click another slot to put it
  down. **Hover** over anything to see its name.
- **Esc** — closes any screen. When in doubt, press Esc.

## 5. Two handy extras

- **B** — the **Inventory Explorer**: a search-everything list of every block,
  tool, and material in the game. Type a few letters to filter.
- **F3** — a debug overlay showing your coordinates (X, Y, Z) and which biome
  you're in. Handy for finding your way back.

## You did it! 🎉

You can move, look, hold things, and open your bag. That's everything you need
to start *doing* stuff.
