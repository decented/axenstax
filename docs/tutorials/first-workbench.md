# Make Your First Workbench

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have turned wood into planks, built a
> **Crafting Table**, placed it, and opened the big **3×3** crafting grid that
> unlocks almost everything.

Your inventory's little **2×2** grid can only make simple things. Tools, a
furnace, a bed — the good stuff — need a **3×3** grid, and you get that by
right-clicking a Crafting Table. So the table is the very first thing to build.

## 1. Get some wood

Walk up to a tree and **hold left-click** on a log until it breaks. Grab at
least **3 or 4 logs**.

## 2. Logs → planks

Press **E** to open your inventory. In the little 2×2 grid:

1. Put **1 log** in any one cell.
2. Click the result — you get **4 planks**.

Do this with a couple of logs so you have plenty of planks.

## 3. Planks → Crafting Table

Still in the 2×2 grid, fill **all four cells with planks**:

```
[P][P]
[P][P]
P = Planks
```

Click the result to get **1 Crafting Table**.

## 4. Place it and open it

1. Close the inventory (**E** or **Esc**).
2. Select the Crafting Table in your hotbar.
3. Aim at the ground and **right-click** to place it.
4. **Right-click the placed table** — now you have a full **3×3** grid (the
   screen even calls it "Workbench").

## 5. Make some sticks while you're here

You'll need sticks for nearly every tool. In either grid, stack **2 planks
vertically**:

```
[P]
[P]
P = Planks  →  4 Sticks
```

## You did it! 🎉

You've got a Crafting Table down and a 3×3 grid open. This is the gateway to the
whole crafting system.
