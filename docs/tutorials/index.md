# Tutorials

Welcome! These are **learn-by-doing** lessons. Each one has a clear goal, a few
numbered steps you can follow exactly, and a "you did it" finish. Never played
before? Start at the top and work down — by the end you'll be mining, crafting,
cooking, surviving the night, farming, and saving your builds.

> **Guide vs Tutorials:** The **[Player Guide](../../player-guide/player-guide/index.md)**
> is for *looking things up* ("what is this block? what does it drop?"). **Tutorials**
> are for *learning to play* ("show me how"). When a tutorial mentions a block, tool,
> or recipe, it links to the Guide if you want the full detail.

## Start here — the beginner path

Do these in order. Each one builds on the last.

1. **[Getting Around](getting-around.md)** — move, look, use your hotbar, open your inventory.
2. **[Your First Blocks](first-blocks.md)** — mine a block, pick it up, place it back down.
3. **[Make Your First Workbench](first-workbench.md)** — wood → planks → a crafting table → the big 3×3 grid.
4. **[Make Your First Tools](first-tools.md)** — wooden tools, then stronger stone tools.
5. **[Cook Food on a Campfire](cook-on-a-campfire.md)** — build it, light it, cook raw meat, eat it.
6. **[Surviving Your First Night](surviving-your-first-night.md)** — light, shelter, and a bed before dark.
7. **[Start Your First Farm](your-first-farm.md)** — till soil, plant seeds, grow and harvest.
8. **[Capture Your First Schematic](capture-a-schematic.md)** — save a little build and rebuild it.

## Building — beyond the cube

Shaped blocks turn a box into a building.

- **[Build with Slabs, Stairs & Doors](build-with-slabs-stairs-and-doors.md)** — lay a half-block slab, run a staircase, and hang a door that opens (plus gates, trapdoors, panes & signs).

## Creating — share your work

- **[Create an Art Gallery](../../player-guide/player-guide/art-galleries.md)** — an artist's full step-by-step: prepare your images, upload them in the Console, hang them on walls with `/exhibit`, run a kiosk, and share a link. Written for non-technical artists (bring a Minecraft-savvy helper for the building bits).
- **[Film a Cinematic Shot](film-a-cinematic-shot.md)** — fly the built-in Director (freecam), hide the HUD, orbit a hero shot, and set up a smooth dolly flythrough you can play back hands-free. *(PC desktop build.)*

## Minigames — how to win

Two quick challenge modes you can start from the in-game chat. Each page is a
clear path to *succeed*.

- **[How to Win Hash Dash](win-hash-dash.md)** — a 3-minute mining sprint for the highest "work" score.
- **[How to Win Satori Rush](win-satori-rush.md)** — race from nothing to your first Satori (the Genesis Block).

## Contraptions — Electricity

Wires, switches, lamps and traps — the "redstone" of Axe'n'Stax.

- **[Your First Circuit](your-first-circuit.md)** — switch a lamp on and off with a lever (source → cable → lamp).
- **[Light-Beam Tripwire](light-beam-tripwire.md)** — a beam you walk through to trigger things; bounce it off mirrors, or use a motion sensor.

## Contraptions — Explosives

Real-chemistry demolition — make Black Powder, build a keg, and blow things up.

- **[Light a Blasting Keg](light-a-blasting-keg.md)** — craft a keg, light the fuse by hand, then detonate it from a distance with a plunger and cables. (Demolition, not mining — a blast gives no drops.)
