# Your First Blocks

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have mined a block, picked it up, and
> placed it back down — and you'll know which tool breaks things fastest.

## 1. Break a block

Point your crosshair at a block close to you (grass, dirt, a tree trunk —
anything within about 4 blocks).

**Hold down left-click.** The block starts to crack, then shatters, and the
item pops into your bag. Let go and aim at the next one.

## 2. Pick it up

You don't have to do anything special — broken blocks are collected
automatically when you're near them. Open your inventory with **E** to see what
you've gathered.

## 3. Place it back down

1. Put the block in your hotbar (it usually goes there on its own) and **select
   its slot** with the number keys or scroll wheel.
2. Aim at the ground or a wall.
3. **Right-click** to place the block against the surface you're pointing at.

Try building a little 2-block-high tower, then break it back down. That loop —
break, carry, place — is the heart of the whole game.

## 4. The right tool is much faster

You *can* break most things with your bare hands, but it's slow, and some blocks
won't give you anything unless you use the proper tool:

- **Pickaxe** → stone and ores
- **Axe** → logs and planks
- **Shovel** → dirt, sand, gravel, snow

Using the matching tool breaks the block **much** faster. And some blocks (like
**stone** and **ores**) need a strong enough pickaxe or they drop **nothing** at
all — see **[Tools Reference](../../player-guide/player-guide/tools-reference.md)**
for which tier you need.

## You did it! 🎉

You can mine, collect, and place blocks. Right now you're using your fists — time
to fix that.
