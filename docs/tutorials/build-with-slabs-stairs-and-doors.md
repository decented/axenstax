# Build with Slabs, Stairs & Doors

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll **lay a slab, run a set of stairs, and hang
> a door you can open and shut** — the three moves that turn a box into a
> building.

Not every block is a full cube. The **shaped blocks** let you build at
half-block detail. The trick to all of them is the same: **where you click, and
which way you're looking, decides how they go down.**

## 1. Get the parts

Easiest while you're learning — open the chat (**T** or **/**) and type:

```
/give slab
/give stairs
/give door
```

(You can also craft them: **3 stone in a row** → 6 **slabs**; 6 stone in a
**staircase** shape → 4 **stairs**; a **2-wide, 3-tall** column of **oak planks**
→ 3 **doors**. The recipe book shows the shapes — search "slab", "stairs",
"door".)

## 2. Lay a slab — and notice the half-block

Look at the **top** of a block on the ground and place a **slab**. See how it
only fills the **bottom half**? Now look at the **underside** of a block above
you and place one — it sticks to the **ceiling**. Click the **middle of a side**
and it stands up as a **vertical slab**.

> 🧠 **The rule:** a slab fills the half **nearest where you clicked**. That's
> all there is to it — top, bottom, or any of the four sides.

## 3. Run a set of stairs

Face the direction you want to **walk up**, then place a **stair**. The step
lands in front of you, facing you. Place another one a block higher and along,
and another — you've got a staircase you can **walk straight up, no jumping**.

> Try placing a stair against the **underside** of a block — it flips
> **upside-down**, perfect for arches and ceiling trim.

## 4. Hang a door

Stand where you want the doorway and place a **door** on the ground — it's **two
blocks tall**, so leave headroom. **Right-click it.** It swings open against the
wall; **right-click again** to shut it.

Now walk through while it's open, then shut it behind you and try to walk through
— you **can't**. A shut door really blocks the way; an open one really lets you
past.

> 🚪 **Double doors:** place a **second door right next to the first** and they
> become a matching pair that opens together — a grand entrance.

## 5. Finish the room

You've got the three core moves. Round it off:

- **Fence gate** (`/give gate`) — the opening in a fence; right-click to open.
- **Trapdoor** (`/give trapdoor`) — a flap for a floor hatch; right-click to open.
- **Glass panes** (`/give pane`) — place a row and watch them **connect** into a
  flat window. Walls (`/give wall`) and iron bars (`/give bars`) connect the same
  way.
- **Sign** (`/give sign`) — place it, right-click to **write** a label, then look
  at it to read it.

> 🎉 **You did it!** Slabs, stairs and a working door are the backbone of every
> build. Everything else in the family — gates, trapdoors, walls, panes, signs,
> frames — places and opens by the same two ideas: **click where you want it**,
> and **right-click the ones that move**. Look anything up in the Guide:
> **[Building Blocks](../../player-guide/building-blocks.md)**.
