# Make Your First Tools

> **Goal:** By the end of this you'll have crafted wooden tools, then upgraded to
> **stone** tools — faster, tougher, and able to mine more.

You'll need your **[Crafting Table](first-workbench.md)** down and some
**sticks** ready. Stand next to the table and **right-click** it to open the 3×3
grid.

## 1. Wooden tools

Tools are made from a **head material** (planks, for wood tier) plus **sticks**.
Here are the shapes — `P` = planks, `S` = stick, `[ ]` = empty:

```
Pickaxe        Axe          Shovel       Sword
[P][P][P]      [P][P]       [P]          [P]
[ ][S][ ]      [P][S]       [S]          [P]
[ ][S][ ]      [ ][S]       [S]          [S]
```

Make at least a **pickaxe** (for stone), an **axe** (for wood), and a **sword**
(for defending yourself tonight). A **shovel** is handy for digging dirt and
sand.

## 2. Mine stone to get cobblestone

Find some stone — it's just under the grass and dirt almost everywhere, or in
any hillside or cave. With your **wooden pickaxe** selected, **hold left-click**
on stone until it breaks. It drops **cobblestone**. Grab a good stack (at least
8–10).

## 3. Stone tools

Make the same tool shapes, but swap the **planks in the head for cobblestone**:

```
Stone Pickaxe
[C][C][C]
[ ][S][ ]
[ ][S][ ]
C = Cobblestone   S = Stick
```

Stone tools mine **faster** and last **far longer** than wood (a wooden pickaxe
breaks after 59 uses; stone lasts 131). You also **need at least a stone
pickaxe** to mine **iron ore** and get anything from it.

## You did it! 🎉

You've got a full set of stone tools. You're now ready to gather almost
everything on the surface — and to start digging for iron.

**What's next:** **[Cook Food on a Campfire](cook-on-a-campfire.md)**, then get
ready for **[your first night](surviving-your-first-night.md)**.
