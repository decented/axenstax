# Campfire — basic cooking primitive (Tier 1.x)

> **Spec 29 status (2026-05-21)** — Campfire is now the **canonical cooking station** for all food. Following Axolittle's playtest, the furnace (Spec 20) was narrowed to ore-only; raw meat → cooked meat happens here, exclusively. The furnace and campfire have no recipe overlap.

**Status**: READY TO BUILD. Phases 2-7 are autonomous solo work. Phase 8 (Axolittle UX playtest) blocks on his time.
**Date**: 2026-05-18
**Branch**: `feat/campfire` once started; off `main`.
**Session**: Fresh — implementer should treat this doc as the only brief.
**Trigger**: 2026-05-18 chat. Removing the current `raw_meat + coal → cooked_meat` crafting-table hack (`crafting.rs:366-377`) — coal-as-kindling is wasteful (coal is also for torches + the future furnace), tonally wrong (a kid expects fire to cook meat, not industrial fuel), and the spec's effort/commerce design principles require cooking to be a *workstation* action, not a one-shot recipe. Campfire is the right replacement: place + fuel + meat + time → cooked.

---

## TL;DR

A placeable Campfire block that takes wood-family fuel (logs / planks / sticks — all available immediately after a player's first tree) and optionally coal (longer burn). Place raw meat on the lit campfire; it cooks over time. Removes the coal-cooking hack from the crafting table when it ships. Doubles as a light source for the kid-friendly "first night" survival moment.

**Fuel ladder** (burn-time per item):

| Fuel | Burn time | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 oak leaf | **1 s** | Kindling — tiny burn, useful for getting friction-fires going |
| 1 stick | 2 s | Kindling+ |
| 1 oak plank | 12 s | Light fuel |
| 1 oak log | 60 s | Standard fuel |
| 1 coal | 240 s | Premium endurance fuel (4× a log) |

**Cooking time**: 10 s per meat at full burn — kid-patience-friendly. Up to 4 meats cooking simultaneously (one per top-face quadrant).

**Fire-starting** is its own beat — *adding fuel does not auto-ignite the campfire*. Two ignition paths:

| Method | Availability | Speed | Reliability | Cost |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Friction** (stick + hold right-click on a fueled unlit campfire) | Day 1 — needs only a stick + a tree | 5 s hold | 70% success, retry on fail | 1 stick durability per attempt |
| **Flint and Steel** (right-click an unlit fueled campfire) | Mid-game — needs flint (gravel drop) + iron ingot | Instant | 100% | 1 tool durability per use (~65 total) |

Friction is the kid-magic option — hold a stick, watch smoke particles intensify, flame pops. Flint+steel is the speed-and-reliability upgrade. Both coexist long-term — friction stays useful when you've run out of flint or your tool broke.

**Why a campfire and not just "cook at the furnace"?** Furnace is Tier 1.5 (industrial — needs iron). Campfire is bronze-age — wood is enough. Players cook their first meat the day they spawn. Both exist long-term: campfire for ambience + outdoor cooking, furnace for scaled production + iron smelting.

Scope: ~550 lines incl. tests. 10 phases. Phase 10 = Axolittle playtest gate.

## Why this lives here

- **Removes a tonally-wrong cooking hack.** The current `crafting.rs:366-377` "raw + coal vertical = cooked" wastes coal (also needed for torches, future furnace) and treats refined fuel as kindling. Kids will expect campfires to cook meat.
- **First-night atmosphere.** A lit campfire becomes the "first night" survival moment — kid spawns, chops a tree, kills a cow, lights a campfire, cooks the first steak as zombies prowl in the dark. That's a real beat that the crafting-table-cooking can't deliver.
- **Pre-T1.5 cooking path.** Furnace lands in T1.5 alongside the workstation framework. Campfire lets players cook *before* T1.5 ships — closing the gap between farming-Tier-1 (now shipped) and processed-economy-Tier-1.5 (still pending) so the cooking arc isn't gated on iron tools.
- **Cross-game lift, per shared infra strategy.** The fuel-burn-down + cook-progress-tick + per-block-entity-state pattern is engine-generic. Any Decented game with a hearth, brazier, kiln, or hot plate can adopt the same mechanic — another game's diner stove, another game's brooder, etc.
- **Sets up the T1.5 block-entity framework without depending on it.** Campfire ships its own minimal `HashMap<(i32, i32, i32), CampfireData>` state. When T1.5's `BlockEntity` trait lands, campfire migrates onto it as a one-line refactor.

## Phase 3 — `CampfireData` state + `World::block_entities`

### Goal

Per-campfire state — fuel remaining, what's cooking, cook-progress — stored alongside the block in a world-level map. Saves and loads with the world.

### Design

```rust
#[derive(Clone, Debug, Serialize, Deserialize)]
pub struct CampfireData {
    /// Ticks of fuel remaining. 0 = unlit. Decrements by 1 per engine tick
    /// while > 0. When this hits 0, the campfire block transitions to
    /// CAMPFIRE_UNLIT (Phase 4 tick handles the transition).
    pub fuel_ticks: u32,
    /// Up to 4 slots; each is None (empty) or Some((raw_item, cook_progress_ticks)).
    /// Slot is "cooked" once cook_progress_ticks >= COOK_TICKS_PER_ITEM (200,
    /// = 10 s at 20 TPS). Mature cooked-item waits in the slot until the
    /// player picks it up (right-click empty hand).
    pub slots: [Option<(crate::item::MaterialId, u32)>; 4],
}

impl CampfireData {
    pub fn is_lit(&self) -> bool { self.fuel_ticks > 0 }
    pub fn empty_slot(&self) -> Option<usize> { self.slots.iter().position(Option::is_none) }
    pub fn add_fuel(&mut self, ticks: u32) { self.fuel_ticks = self.fuel_ticks.saturating_add(ticks); }
}
```

- `World` gains `pub block_entities: AHashMap<(i32, i32, i32), CampfireData>`. Initialised empty. Looked up on demand.
- `save::WorldSave` gains an optional `campfires: Vec<(i32, i32, i32, CampfireData)>` field. Bincode-positional, append at the end.
- Old saves: missing field defaults to an empty Vec. World loads with no campfires (correct — old saves predate this feature).

### Fuel value table (in `campfire.rs`)

```rust
/// Burn-time per fuel item, in 20-TPS ticks.
pub fn fuel_value(material: Option<MaterialId>, block: Option<BlockId>) -> Option<u32> {
    if let Some(b) = block {
        match b {
            block::OAK_LEAVES => Some(1 * 20),   // 1 s — fastest kindling
            block::OAK_LOG => Some(60 * 20),     // 60 s — standard fuel
            block::OAK_PLANKS => Some(12 * 20),  // 12 s — light fuel
            _ => None,
        }
    } else if let Some(m) = material {
        match m {
            MaterialId::Stick => Some(2 * 20),   // 2 s — kindling+
            MaterialId::Coal => Some(240 * 20),  // 240 s — premium fuel
            _ => None,
        }
    } else { None }
}
```

The leaf-as-fuel role: kindling. 1 s of burn won't cook a steak (needs 10 s) but it WILL keep a campfire lit through a friction-ignition + a few seconds of warm-up. Players who've stripped a tree and accumulated leaves can use them to "get the fire going" before adding logs for sustained cook-time.

- `campfire::tests::fuel_value_for_known_items` — sticks, planks, logs, coal all return the right burn time.

## Phase 4 — Fuel burn-down + cook-progress tick

### Goal

Every game tick, every campfire with fuel decrements its fuel by 1, advances any cooking items by 1, transitions to/from lit state as fuel runs out.

### Design

Pure free function `campfire::tick(world, tick) -> Vec<BlockChange>` matching `growth::advance_crops`. Iterates `world.block_entities`. For each entry:

1. If `fuel_ticks > 0`: decrement by 1; advance any cooking slots by 1.
2. If a slot's cook_progress hits `COOK_TICKS_PER_ITEM` (200): convert the raw item to its cooked variant in place. Item stays in the slot until the player picks it up.
3. If `fuel_ticks == 0`: transition block to `CAMPFIRE_UNLIT`. Pending cooking pauses (slots keep their state — player can re-light to resume).
4. If `fuel_ticks > 0` and the block is currently `CAMPFIRE_UNLIT`: transition to `CAMPFIRE` (lit). (Happens when fuel is added to an unlit campfire — Phase 5.)

Block transitions go through the standard `BlockChange` broadcast path.

```rust
const COOK_TICKS_PER_ITEM: u32 = 200;  // 10 s @ 20 TPS

const fn cooked_variant(raw: MaterialId) -> Option<MaterialId> {
    match raw {
        MaterialId::RawBeef => Some(MaterialId::CookedBeef),
        MaterialId::RawPorkchop => Some(MaterialId::CookedPorkchop),
        MaterialId::RawChicken => Some(MaterialId::CookedChicken),
        MaterialId::RawMutton => Some(MaterialId::CookedMutton),
        _ => None,
    }
}
```

### Wiring

Call `campfire::tick` once per engine tick from `game_loop.rs` in the same place `growth::advance_crops` is called. BRIDGE comment: single-player owns the tick until HostedServer routing lands.

## Phase 5 — Right-click interactions (fuel + meat + pickup)

### Goal

Hold fuel → right-click adds fuel to the campfire. Hold raw meat → right-click places on first empty slot. Empty-hand right-click on a slot with cooked meat → pick up.

**Adding fuel does NOT auto-ignite.** The campfire stays in its lit/unlit state regardless of fuel-add. Ignition is Phase 6.

### Changes

In `game_loop.rs`'s right-click handler, add a new branch *before* the bonemeal-on-grass branch (since campfire has higher priority than the generic block-place):

1. If `target_blk == CAMPFIRE || target_blk == CAMPFIRE_UNLIT` and the player holds an item:
   - If fuel item (oak leaf / stick / plank / log / coal): consume 1 from hand, add to campfire's `fuel_ticks`. Block state unchanged — an unlit campfire stays unlit even after adding fuel. (Phase 6 ignites it; if already lit, fuel just stacks.)
   - Else if raw meat AND there's an empty slot: consume 1 from hand, place in first empty slot. Meat sits with `cook_progress = 0` until the fire is actually lit + burning.
   - Else if empty hand AND there's a cooked-meat slot: pick up the cooked meat into player inventory; clear the slot. If the player's inventory is full, toast `"Inventory full"` (item stays in the slot — player has to make space).
   - Else: fall through to default behaviour.

Place cooldown: 4 ticks per interaction (allow rapid log-stacking).

## Phase 6 — Fire-starting (friction + flint-and-steel)

### Goal

Two ignition paths for a fueled unlit campfire. Friction is free + slow + can fail; flint-and-steel is fast + reliable + costs iron.

### Friction ignition (stick + hold)

When the player right-clicks an unlit **fueled** campfire with a stick in hand, start a hold-to-ignite gesture:

- **Hold duration**: 100 ticks (5 s @ 20 TPS).
- **During hold**: spawn friction-smoke particles at the campfire's top face (intensity ramps up over the hold). Right-mouse must stay held; releasing aborts.
- **At completion**: roll once against 70% success rate.
  - Success → set block to `CAMPFIRE` (lit). Audio cue. Toast (first time only — `"Friction fire started!"`).
  - Failure → toast `"The stick wasn't dry enough."` — player can retry immediately. No state change beyond stick durability.
- **Cost**: 1 stick durability per attempt (success or failure). Sticks have no durability today — for friction, treat each stick as a 1-use kindling consumable: success or fail, the stick is gone. Honest about real-world physics (you'd snap the bow-drill spindle).
- **Unfueled campfire**: hold mechanic refuses with toast `"The fire needs fuel first."`

The 70% fail rate is deliberately generous — fire-starting should feel like a skill check, not a wall. Phase 10 playtest tunes; default starting value is 70%.

> **2026-06-22 realignment + guided-feedback polish (playtest fix).** The friction path had drifted from the "unfuelled refuses" rule above: a 2026-05-19 change let a stick light an *empty* campfire, which the fuel-burn tick then snuffed out on the very next tick — the owner reported *"lit it, got the band, but it just stayed cold."* Fix: friction now gates on `campfire::can_ignite(Option<&CampfireData>)` (true iff `fuel_ticks > 0`); striking an unfuelled campfire toasts *"Add fuel first (wood), then strike to light."* without starting the hold or consuming the stick. Flint-and-steel already gated on fuel (it never extinguishes-after-light in practice — §"Flint and Steel" line about empty-ignite is now superseded by the shared gate). Plus three feedback fixes the same report drove: (1) the hover panel gained a **"Ready to light"** state for unlit-but-fuelled campfires (it previously read "Cold" and hid the fuel the player had just added); (2) the panel now shows **per-slot cooking lines** (`Cooking: Raw Beef — 45%` / `Ready: Cooked Beef`) so meat is never invisible; (3) placing raw meat toasts *"On the fire — cooking…"* or *"…light it to start cooking"*. Pure helpers `campfire::can_ignite` + `campfire_ui::campfire_hover_text` are unit-tested. Spec 05 §3.10 updated. No save-format / block / item change.

### Flint and Steel ignition

New item + tool:

- **`MaterialId::Flint`** (appended after farming materials). Dropped from `GRAVEL` blocks at 15% per break (matches Minecraft baseline). Update `block::mine_drop` for gravel to roll the chance.
- **`ToolType::FlintAndSteel`** (appended after Hoe). Recipe: 1 flint + 1 iron ingot in a 2×1 vertical pattern (flint top, iron bottom — Minecraft pattern). Single-tier (no wood/stone/diamond variants — flint-and-steel is its own thing). Durability: 65 uses (Minecraft baseline).
- **Use**: right-click an unlit campfire (fueled or not — flint-and-steel can ignite even an empty campfire, but then it instantly extinguishes for lack of fuel; player learns to fuel first).
  - Set block to `CAMPFIRE` (lit), if there's fuel.
  - Decrement tool durability by 1.
  - Audio: flint-strike spark sound.
  - Visual: brief spark particle effect.
- **Future** (post-T1): generic fire-lighting on any solid block top face for the future fire-spread system. Out of scope here.

### Changes

- `item.rs`: append `MaterialId::Flint` (after the Wave 26 farming materials — bincode-safe). Add name, colour (light grey, slightly translucent feel), `food_value: None`. Add to `entity_model::material_texture` mapping (new texture layer).
- `crafting.rs`: append `ToolType::FlintAndSteel` (after `Hoe`). Recipe in `match_recipe`: 1×2 vertical (flint over iron ingot) → 1 FlintAndSteel tool. Durability via `max_durability(material)` — but FlintAndSteel is single-tier; either add a special-case 65-durability arm or accept a new constant `FLINT_AND_STEEL_DURABILITY: u16 = 65`.
- `game_loop.rs` right-click handler: extend the campfire branch from Phase 5 with the two ignition paths. The friction path needs a per-player hold-timer state (`friction_hold_ticks: u32`) on `PlayerSlot`; right-click-held increments it, right-release resets it, hitting 100 triggers the success roll.
- `block.rs`: extend `GRAVEL`'s drop table to roll flint at 15%. Existing GRAVEL block stays the same; only the drop table changes.
- `texture_gen.rs`: new flint icon (small grey shard, 1 texture layer).
- `audio.rs`: friction-rasp loop (plays during hold), flint-strike spark, fire-whoosh on ignition.

## Tier 1.5+ sketch

Campfire and Furnace coexist long-term:

| Feature | Campfire | Furnace (T1.5) |
|---|---|---|
| **Recipe gate** | Wood only (3 sticks + 3 logs + 3 sticks) | Iron-tier (requires iron ingots — has to be obtained somehow before furnace exists; chicken-and-egg if iron smelting moves to furnace) |
| **Cooks meat** | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Smelts iron** | ✗ — pure cooking | ✓ — primary purpose |
| **Light source** | ✓ (level 14) | ✗ (some glow but not a primary light source) |
| **Outdoor ambience** | ✓ — designed for it | ✗ — industrial |
| **Block-entity framework** | minimal HashMap (this spec) | full BlockEntity trait (T1.5) |

**Iron-furnace chicken-and-egg**: if the furnace recipe requires iron ingots and iron smelting moves *to* the furnace, players can't craft the first furnace. Solutions:
- (a) Keep iron smelting at the crafting table (the BRIDGE) until a future tier introduces a non-furnace iron path (campfire-with-coal-and-iron-ore? a smelter mob?).
- (b) Build the first furnace with raw iron (no ingot needed).
- (c) The campfire can smelt iron if you add coal to it (extends the campfire's role; tonally fine — fire is fire).
- The T1.5 spec needs to make this call; not this campfire spec's problem to solve.

## Open questions

- **Can raw meat be placed on an unlit campfire?** Spec defaults to "yes — sits there with cook_progress 0 until lit". Player UX: less confusing than refusing.
- **Multiple cooks at the same time on a single campfire** — 4 slots. Visually how do we distinguish 4 items? Top texture quadrants? Stacked icons? Or just one "stuff is cooking" texture for all 4? Alpha can ship with a single "cooking" indicator and add multi-slot visualisation as polish.
- **Friction success rate** — 70% is the starting value. Phase 10 playtest tunes. Lower (50%) makes flint-and-steel feel more powerful; higher (85%) makes friction more pleasant but flint-and-steel less of an upgrade. 70% is the deliberate middle.
- **Friction hold duration** — 5 s. Long enough to feel like an action; short enough that retrying isn't punishment. Could be 3 s (snappier) or 8 s (more weighty). Playtest call.
- **Sticks consumed on friction failure** — yes by default ("you snapped the spindle"). Alternative is "no consumption on failure" which would be more forgiving. Honest design says yes, kid-friendly design says no. Default to yes; playtest decides.
- **Flint drop rate from gravel** — 15% per Minecraft baseline. Could go higher (25%) if gravel feels too scarce for the iron-tier player who's just looking to make their first F&S.
- **Leaf-drop-on-decay rate** — 25% per decayed leaf. Tunable. Too high and leaf farms trivialise kindling; too low and players never accumulate leaves.
- **Campfire extinguished by water/rain?** Future weather system territory. Skip for alpha — campfires can be placed under cover at the player's discretion.
- **Stacked fuel cap?** A campfire with 50 logs added at once has 50 minutes of fuel. Should there be a maximum? Probably not — let players over-fuel and walk away, comes back to a still-lit campfire.
- **Lit campfire collision** — solid block? Walkable? Minecraft makes campfires solid with a top face the player can stand on. Default to that (cosmetic + simple); revisit if the "stand on the fire and don't burn" looks silly. Damage-on-stand is a future polish.
- **Charcoal as a fuel** (Minecraft has it as the "cooked log" output of a wood-fired furnace). Out of scope for Tier 1.x; lands in T4 preservation if at all.
