## TL;DR

A purple ostrich (the Nostr mascot) that lives in Savanna biomes, lays eggs equal to **21 chicken eggs** by yield (the Bitcoin nod), sheds purple feathers, is **tameable via berries**, and is **protected by a curse mechanic** — kill or eat one and you trigger **The Nostrich's Vow**, a multi-system economic penalty that lasts ~24 real-minutes (1 in-game day) until purified.

Three threads:

1. **Mob + ecology** — passive-flee + retaliate-kick AI, sprint-faster speed, family groups of 2-3, Savanna-only spawn. Reuses the tameable framework from `2026-05-20-wolves-tameable-companion.md`.
2. **Egg + feather economy** — one Nostrich Egg unlocks T5-tier premium recipes; feathers go into premium arrows + decorative dye + a high trade-value commodity slot.
3. **Curse mechanic** — kill triggers `nostrich_vow` on the killer's `PlayerSlot`. Vow blocks vendor purchases, zeroes village rep, suppresses sats payouts. Purification via memorial-block placement OR wait-it-out.

Cross-game lift: the mascot-protection pattern lifts directly to other games on the same primitives (per launch scope axenstax only), whichever mascot species they use. Building it here validates the shared-infra primitives.

---

## Why a Nostrich, and why this design

**Mascot identity**. AxeNStax already runs on Nostr (Signet auth, Charter, the relay). Embedding the Nostr mascot in the game world — and making it sacred-cow — is thematically aligned with the underlying tech: hurting the mascot has economic consequences via the same Nostr/Bitcoin stack the player otherwise benefits from.

**21 eggs = the Bitcoin nod**. A real ostrich egg ≈ 24 chicken eggs by volume; the gameplay yield clamps to **21** as a deliberate reference to the 21-million-BTC cap. The number is locked, not playtest-tunable, because it's the joke.

**The protection mechanic is the creative core**. A naive "killing the mascot is forbidden" rule is boring. A graduated curse with multiple purification paths is fun, educational, and gives players agency: they can kill a Nostrich (it's not blocked), they just see the consequences and learn to either avoid it or atone for it. Kid-friendly tone (a flavour toast, not a system error). Cross-multiplayer storytelling (memorials in shared worlds).

## Scope (what's in)

### A. The Nostrich mob

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `MobType::Nostrich` | New variant, appended for save-compat |
| Category | Passive (until provoked) |
| Width × Height | 0.6 × 2.1 (taller than horse, narrower than cow) |
| Colour | `[0.55, 0.35, 0.75]` — Nostr-mascot purple |
| Health | 20 (between Cow 10 and Horse 20) |
| Speed | 8.0 blocks/sec (faster than player sprint at ~6) |
| Drops on natural death | 1-3 NostrichFeather |
| Drops on player kill | 1-3 NostrichFeather + 0-1 RawNostrichMeat **+ triggers vow** |
| Spawn biome | Savanna only |
| Spawn weight | 5 (rare-but-present; horses still dominate Savanna at 35) |
| Group size | 2-3 (family unit; deterministic on first spawn) |

Data file: `data/mobs/nostrich.toml`. Texture-gen: a new procedural sprite layer per the existing mob-texture pattern (purple body, slim neck, comical legs).

### B. AI behaviour

Reuses the tameable framework (`tameable::OwnershipData`). State machine:

| State | Trigger | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `Idle` | Default | Wanders slowly within 8 blocks of spawn-anchor. Stops to peck the ground occasionally. |
| `Flee` | Took damage from a player OR a hostile mob within 5 blocks | Sprint away at full 8.0 b/s for 5 seconds, then drop back to Idle if safe. |
| `Kick` | Player attacks within reach (≤ 2 blocks) | One-frame retaliate attack: 6 damage + small knockback. Then transition to `Flee`. |
| `Charge` | Cornered (fled into a wall, can't escape ≥ 2 seconds) | Charge the attacker at full speed for 1.5 s; double damage on contact. Then `Flee`. |
| `Follow` | Tamed, owner > 8 blocks away | Trot to within 4 blocks of owner. |
| `Sit` | Tamed, owner right-clicked with empty hand | Stay put; don't follow. |

Untamed Nostriches default to Idle. Tamed Nostriches default to Follow.

### C. Taming

**Mechanic**: hand-feed Mixed Berries × N (recommend `N = 7` — the lucky number; tunable post-playtest). Each successful feed has a 1-in-3 chance to tame; expected ~21 feeds to tame at the high tail.

Implementation: each feed calls `tameable::attempt_tame_generic(&mut ownership, &owner_pubkey, seed, 1, 3)`. On success, sets `owner_pubkey` and the Nostrich's state transitions to Follow. Cancels the curse mechanic — a Nostrich that's been tamed by player X is "their" Nostrich, not the wild Nostr mascot.

**Failure mode**: feeding consumes the berry regardless of tame outcome. Once tamed, additional berries are ignored (no over-feed exploit).

### D. Egg-laying

Tamed Nostriches lay eggs every **24,000 ticks** (1 in-game day at default 1× time-speed). The egg drops as a loose `ItemEntity` (NostrichEgg material) at the Nostrich's current position. Stack-size 1 per egg (each one is precious).

Per-mob timer: `nostrich.lay_ticks_remaining: u32`, decremented per tick when tamed. On reach 0 → spawn item + reset to 24,000.

Untamed Nostriches do **not** lay eggs in the world — they presumably lay in hidden nests and incubate. Mechanically this means players have to tame to access the egg economy. Reinforces the "treat Nostriches well" loop.

**Recipes consuming NostrichEgg**:

- **Nostrich Omelette** — NostrichEgg + 1 wheat flour + (any vegetable) in any 3-slot pattern. Restores 12 hunger + 8 saturation. Trade-value 80 (T4-tier).
- **Royal Pavlova** — NostrichEgg + 3 sugar + 1 berries. Restores 14 hunger + 6 saturation. Trade-value 120 (T5-tier).
- **Nostrich Custard** — NostrichEgg + 1 milk + 2 sugar. Restores 10 hunger + 10 saturation. Trade-value 70.

(All three slot into the Farming Vision §6 recipe ladder at the T5 specialty tier.)

### E. Feather production

Tamed Nostriches shed **1 NostrichFeather** every **48,000 ticks** (2 in-game days). Untamed Nostriches also shed naturally, but the feather drops at the mob's anchor not collected by anyone — players who happen to be near a wild Nostrich at the right tick can pick one up.

**Recipes consuming NostrichFeather**:

- **Nostrich Arrow** — 1 stick (top) + 1 flint (middle) + 1 NostrichFeather (bottom), vertical pattern. Output: 6 Nostrich Arrows. Damage +20% vs regular arrow, range +20%. (Standard arrow recipe today is stick + flint + chicken feather → 4 arrows.)
- **Purple Banner** — 6 cloth + 1 NostrichFeather, banner pattern. Decorative; trade-value 25.
- **Decorative dye** (deferred) — banner-style dyeing using crushed feather. Trade-value as a commodity.

### F. The Nostrich's Vow (curse mechanic)

**Trigger**: A player's attack kills a Nostrich (entity reaches 0 HP from the player's last damage). Hostile-mob kills DO NOT trigger the vow (it was already dying — the player isn't the cause).

**Storage**: `PlayerSlot.nostrich_vow: Option<NostrichVow>` where:

```rust
pub struct NostrichVow {
    pub ticks_remaining: u32,  // Default: 24_000 (1 in-game day)
    pub origin_tick: u64,       // For audit logging
}
```

**Effects while active** (`nostrich_vow.is_some()`):

| System | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Vendor Block buy | Refused with toast "The Nostriches will not aid your dealings." |
| Village reputation | All village rep reads return `Tier::Hostile` regardless of stored score. Quest-givers refuse to issue new quests. |
| `apply_sats_payout` | Returns `PayoutResult { suppressed: true, .. }`. Hashes still emit (Proof-of-Play stays educational); settlement is held. |
| Hostile mob aggro | Mobs sense the vow and pursue more aggressively (+50% aggro radius). The Nostriches' kin take note. |

**Decay**: `ticks_remaining` counts down per game tick. On reach 0, the vow ends; rep + vendor + sats return to normal.

**Purification paths**:

1. **Build a Nostrich Memorial**. Place a 3×3×1 pattern of **purple wool** with **1 NostrichEgg** on the centre + **3 NostrichFeather** at the cardinal-edge tiles. On placement of the final piece, the game scans for a complete memorial within 8 blocks of the player; if found, vow ends instantly + a permanent `NostrichMemorial` block-entity marks the spot for future visitors. Cross-multiplayer storytelling.
2. **Wait it out** — 24,000 ticks decay naturally.
3. **Tribute** (Bitcoin-enabled servers only, deferred to a follow-up spec) — spend N sats via a future "Tribute" UI to clear the vow instantly. Hooks into `apply_sats_payout` via a new `PayoutKind::NostrichTribute`. Not in this spec's scope — Phase G ships only the memorial path; the tribute path is open work.

**Eating Nostrich meat (raw or cooked)**:

- Heals only 1 hunger + 0 saturation (tough and sour).
- Eating any cooked or raw Nostrich meat triggers a fresh vow even if you didn't kill it (e.g., picked it up from a Nostrich killed by a hostile mob).
- One "Tongue-of-the-Slayer" item drops into the eater's inventory on consumption. Sticky — can't be discarded normally; removed by the memorial purification flow alongside the vow. Educational + memorable. **DEFERRED — not in this spec; just the vow + 1-hunger food-value for v1**.

### G. Memorial block

`NostrichMemorial` block-entity, placed automatically by the memorial-detection scan. Stores:

```rust
pub struct NostrichMemorialData {
    pub placer_pubkey: String,   // Who built it (for cross-multiplayer attribution)
    pub placed_at_tick: u64,
    pub message: Option<String>, // Optional inscription, deferred
}
```

Visually a small purple plaque on top of the centre wool block. Right-click shows attribution: "Built by [npub-shortened] on [date]. The Nostriches remember."

Memorial blocks are **permanent fixtures** — they don't decay. Mining one with any pickaxe destroys it (no drops); the wool blocks beneath remain.

### H. Curse HUD + flavour toasts

**On vow trigger**:

- Big amber toast for ~5 seconds: `"The Nostriches will remember this..."`
- Smaller follow-up toast: `"Vow active. Memorials purify, time heals. Vendor + village + sats temporarily blocked."`

**While vow active**:

- HUD corner badge (purple feather icon + countdown timer in real-minutes)
- Tooltip on hover: "The Nostrich's Vow. [N min remaining] until natural decay."

**On purification**:

- Toast: `"The Nostriches' Vow is lifted. Welcome back."`

**On vendor/village/sats refusal under vow**:

- One-line toast explaining why ("The Nostriches will not aid your dealings.")
- Toast cooldown to avoid spam (1 per 5 seconds).

## Open design questions

1. **Tame rate**: 1-in-3 per berry-feed → expected ~21 feeds for the 95th-percentile. Too patient for a kid? Drop to 1-in-2 or even 1-in-1 for the first feed in a clutch? Recommend 1-in-3 with a "first feed has 1-in-2 because it's new + cool" tweak post-playtest.
2. **Egg lay rate**: 1 per in-game day = once per real-72-minutes at default 1× time-speed. Too slow? Consider 1 per 12,000 ticks (half day) for v1 to give the kid quick payoff during a single play session.
3. **Vow severity**: blocking vendor + village + sats simultaneously might feel punishing. Test scenarios:
   - Player accidentally kills a Nostrich during combat practice → punished?
   - Player kills a Nostrich for the meat once to see what happens → recoverable?
4. **Memorial wool colour**: purple wool requires dye. Dye system isn't shipped yet. v1 substitutes plain wool + an inscription on the memorial block; once dye lands, the purple-wool requirement upgrades.
5. **Spawn rarity**: weight 5 in Savanna might still be too common given a 200×200 patch. Recommend 5 → 3 if Axolittle says he sees too many.
6. **Family group cohesion**: when a player attacks one Nostrich, do the others in the group also flee? Recommend yes — kin-fear is part of the design's emotional weight.
7. **Cross-server slayer tag** — Charter relay event publication of `nostrich_killed`. Decoupled from this spec but worth confirming the wire-shape for follow-up.

## Cross-game lift

- **Mascot-protection mechanism** is generic — any sibling game's own mascot species gets the same pattern.
- **Tameable-mob-with-curse-on-death** lifts to other tameables (cats, parrots) — even if their curse is milder.
- **Memorial-purification block-entity scan** is a reusable detection primitive — any future "build this 3D shape to trigger a state change" feature (altars, shrines, trophies) uses the same scan.
- **A sibling game built on this primitive** — when it thaws, the entire Nostrich-as-livestock loop (tame, lay, feather-shed, mascot protection) is already done. Day-zero infrastructure win.
